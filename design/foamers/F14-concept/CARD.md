## Why the roster needs it

The elites teach distinct skills and none teaches this one:

- F10 Turbo: prioritising targets (jets).
- F11 Seawall: parry mastery (shield).
- F12 Ambusher: clearing growths, awareness.
- F13 Biomechanic: target priority (tank), healing.

What is missing: an elite that punishes the greedy finisher and teaches the
spacing, stagger, finisher rhythm. The Warden holds 3 to 4 m away and slams
the floor in a wide ring, takes darts only through the legs, staggers for
about 2.0 s after the legs break, and is finished with the melee or B03 in
that window. Players who greed a finisher from the middle of the arena eat
the slam; players who hold range and break the legs get rewarded. No other
Foamer gives this lesson, and no F05-style heavy does.

Breakable parts (proposal, matching section 3.1): legs, arms, head. Most
useful part to break: legs (it limps; at a quarter speed with both legs
broken).

## How the player beats it with the existing blasters and parry

Existing, no new blasters:

- Keep distance (3+ m) and use cover. Darts full reach do not pierce
  through the body; aim at the legs, which are bright orange (weak point).
- Heavy darts (B06): thrown at the legs, they knock the Warden toward its
  knees; a leg break staggers it ~2.0 s.
- Break action (B03): point-blank on the legs, a chunk pops out of the leg
  mass and the Warden goes down.
- Parry: the close-in smash (pink, 0.8 s wind-up) is parryable with the
  dodge-or-parry commitment. A clean parry leaves the Warden staggered,
  opening the finisher window. This is the elite version of F11's lesson,
  different enough to teach a new thing: spacing + parry + finisher.
- Finisher: in the 2.0 s stagger (after legs break or right after a parry,
  per F11's 2.5 s stagger proposal) use the melee finisher or B03; the
  finisher always pops the Warden.

## How it reads as the same species

Same one species as every Foamer: rounded blocky voxel forms, the same three
primaries (purple, orange, lime) in the same factory lineage, body lime
armour + deep purple frame + orange limbs as the weak point, ink visor band
with two cream eyes, no teeth, no black mouth, no horror, no wings or fins.
Deep shades per the tier rule. The broad shoulders, squat legs and large
head give it an F11-adjacent but distinct silhouette (F11 is the armed
sumo-knight; F14 is a wider, lower, unhurried one with a maul instead of a
shield). The deep-purple-and-lime-armor read makes it look like the heaviest
non-boss in act 3.

## Catastrophic-death idea (in the spirit of D12)

D12 asks for more crammed-together death varieties beyond "the whole Foamer
explodes": Paul's example is a close-range blast with B03 that ends a
heavily-weakened elite by tearing out a big chunk of the chest plate, not by
popping the whole body.

F14's catastrophic death: a heavily-weakened Warden (40%+ health in one
hit, or a point-blank B03/B06 on the legs) is not popped whole. The lime
chest plate peels off as one big foam chunk, one orange leg stump is blown
off as a second chunk, the Warden buckles forward onto its knees, and the
two chunks settle and shrink away. No whole-body explosion. Audio: a deep,
low thud, not a pop. This reads as the destruction of something big, not a
fodder pop.
