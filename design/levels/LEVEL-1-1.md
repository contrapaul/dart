# Level 1-1 concept

Status: **proposal, LVL-00**. Works in any setting; place names are
[Paul]. First level of Act 1.

| | |
|---|---|
| New blaster | B01 starter (CONTENT-PLAN 2) |
| New Foamer | F01 Slowmer, 6 health, bonk only |
| New mechanic | Move, shoot, carve cover |
| Arenas | 3, plus a bridge set piece and a gauntlet |
| Target time | 17 min (15 to 20) |
| Archetypes | A1 Pit, A4 Long hall, A8 Bridge, A3 Bowl, A6 Yard |

## 1. What it teaches, and how the space does it

The level has to teach four things with no text: you move fast, darts
hurt Foamers, foam walls are made of foam, and the bright way is the way
on.

- **Shooting is safe here.** The first Slowmer is alone in a 10 x 8 m
  side room at 10 m, standing still, facing away. One shot that misses
  costs nothing: the room has a back wall 8 m behind the player.
- **Cover is destructible.** Arena 1 is a pit with a 6 x 4 x 3.5 m foam
  block in the middle. Wave 1 is 3 Slowmers, and the player will shoot
  through the block by accident. When it breaks in two shots, the block
  falls apart and the lesson lands without a word.
- **Foam plugs the way.** Arena 2 ends in a 3.2 x 3.2 x 1 m plug of
  frozen foam in the only doorway. The player has already seen foam
  break, so the plug reads as "shoot it". This is the level's one gated
  destructible.
- **The way on is lit.** Every exit is the brightest surface in its room,
  and the exit door of every arena is on the far wall from the entrance.

Difficulty curve on Normal: max 2 Slowmers attacking at once, 8 alive at
most. Nothing here can kill a player who keeps moving.

## 2. Beat by beat

| # | Beat | Space | Minutes | What the player does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open | Loading yard, 20 x 16 m | 1.5 | Walks, sprints, jumps a 1.2 m kerb. B01 on a crate with a full reserve tile beside it (A1). One Slowmer visible through a window, not yet reachable |
| 2 | Explore 1 | Corridor 12 m, then a 10 x 8 m side room | 1.5 | Kills the lone Slowmer. The corridor's side wall is a **broken wall** (S1). A sign of movement behind it, no text |

| 4 | Breather | Overlook, 8 x 6 m | 1.0 | Station U1, ammo cache A2, a window looking back into the pit (so the player sees the block they broke) |
| 5 | Arena 2 | A4 Long hall, 18 x 44 m | 2.5 | Two waves down a 44 m lane, columns to sidestep behind. After the waves the far door is sealed by a foam plug: shoot it |
| 6 | Set piece | A8 Bridge and drop | 1.5 | Stair up 5 m, a 4 m wide bridge over a 9 m drop, a Slowmer on the bridge that can push you off. Drop into Arena 3, no way back |
| 7 | Arena 3 | A3 Tiered bowl, 36 x 30 m | 2.5 | 6 then 8 Slowmers from three rifts on the upper tiers. Height is the lesson: shoot down, or climb to break line of sight |

Secrets add 2 to 4 minutes for a hunter. Total: **17 min**.

## 4. Arenas

### Arena 1: Pit (A1)

- **Footprint:** 34 x 26 m, pit floor 4 m below the entry ring, 12 m
  ceiling, two 6 m ramps at the short ends.
- **Lock:** trigger 8 m in, door behind closes, two side shutters close
  as well so the player cannot hug the entry ledge.
- **Waves:**
  1. 3 Slowmer, rifts 1 and 2 (north-west and north-east). Slow: 25 s.
  2. 5 Slowmer, same rifts plus rift 3 (floor, centre-south). 40 s.
- **Destructibles:** centre block, 3 zones (6 x 4 x 3.5 m of foam-orange
  cover), 2 shots per zone from B01. Two 1.6 x 1.6 x 2.8 m pillars on the
  ring, 1 zone each, positioned so one falls by accident.
- **Why it works:** the player can see the whole fight from the entry
  ring for a full 3 seconds before the lock closes. The pit reads as a
  stage. Nothing can flank, so the player learns to shoot.
- **Ammo:** A2 on the south ring, 4 darts.
- **Exit:** north-west stairs, a lit lift shaft, visible from the entry.

### Arena 2: Long hall (A4)

- **Footprint:** 18 x 44 m, 7 m ceiling, six 1.2 x 1.2 x 4 m columns in
  two staggered rows at 8 m spacing.
- **Lock:** trigger at the third column, 14 m in, 4 m past the first
  cover.
- **Waves:**
  1. 4 Slowmer from the far end (one rift, north wall), walking 30 m.
  2. 6 Slowmer, rift north plus one rift in the east wall at 22 m.
- **Destructibles:** two columns are voxel (plastic, hardness 1.5, one
  zone each) and two are shell (bedrock). The player cannot tell which
  until they shoot, which is the point: cover wears down, structure does
  not. After the waves, the north door is plugged by a 3.2 x 3.2 x 1 m
  foam plug (1 zone, 3 to 5 shots, or one melee).
- **Why it works:** 44 m of straight line is the longest sight line in
  the level and the only place where the player can backpedal while
  shooting, which teaches that walking backwards is allowed.
- **Exit:** through the plug, then stairs up.

### Arena 3: Tiered bowl (A3)

- **Footprint:** 36 x 30 m, tiers at 0, 1.2 and 2.4 m, 9 m ceiling.
- **Lock:** trigger 10 m in on the bottom tier. The entry is on tier 1,
  so the player walks down 1.2 m to start, which is free (below the jump).
- **Waves:**
  1. 6 Slowmer from rifts on tier 2 (two rifts, 14 m apart).
  2. 8 Slowmer, tier 2 rifts plus a floor rift behind the player.
- **Destructibles:** three 1.6 x 1.6 x 2.8 m cover pillars on tier 1
  (one zone each), and one floor island 3.2 x 0.4 x 3.2 m on tier 2 that
  drops when shot, opening a hole to tier 0. The hole is a shortcut, not
  the critical path.
- **Why it works:** the first time a Slowmer is above the player, the
  player has to look up. Nothing punishes them for it here.
- **Exit:** a lit doorway on tier 0's north wall, the darkest place in
  the room, with the brightest frame. The contrast is the cue.

### Gauntlet: Yard with islands (A6)

- **Footprint:** 44 x 36 m, 9 m ceiling, six 4 to 6 m destructible
  islands, 2.5 m tall, gaps 5 to 6.5 m (all sprint jumpable).
- **Lock:** the lift alcove is visible the whole time and stays shut
  until the gauntlet clears.
- **Waves** (no lock release between them, the rifts pulse again):
  1. 8 Slowmer, rifts at both short ends.
  2. 10 Slowmer, same rifts plus a floor rift in the centre. The islands
     lose 2 to 4 of their 6 zones by now, so the space is more open than
     it was.
- **Destructibles:** six islands, 1 to 2 zones each, foam-orange. Cap:
  never more than 6 zones in this arena at once, per KIT 5.
- **Ammo:** A4 and A5, 6 darts each, at the two long ends. A player who
  backs into a corner finds ammo, which is the D08 rule: place it, do not
  drop it.
- **Exit:** the lift, which is the brightest thing in the level, 3 m
  wide, with a light that pulses once when the gauntlet clears.

## 5. Secrets (3)

| # | Type (KIT 4) | Where | Hint | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Broken wall | Explore 1, west wall of the corridor where the path bends | A crack decal and a dart thunk sound; the wall is a shade lighter | 20 darts, and a shortcut straight into Arena 1's ring |
| S2 | Drop behind the fight | Bridge set piece, a 2.5 m hole in the near yard's floor | Foam scraped on the rim, a light 9 m below | Upgrade token 1, plus a 2.1 m vent back to the bridge |
| S3 | Ledge you can just reach | Breather 2, 1.5 m lip above eye height | Uplight on the lip, an ammo box visible on it | Collectible toy 1 |

None is inside a lock-in, per README 3.
