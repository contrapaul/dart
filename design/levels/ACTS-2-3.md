# Acts 2 and 3: short concepts

Status: **proposal, LVL-00**. One paragraph per level, with the new thing
(CONTENT-PLAN table 2), the signature space, the archetypes (KIT.md) and
one sketch of the signature arena. Settings, place names and story are
[Paul]. Every level is 15 to 20 minutes, bosses about 5.

Archetype codes: A1 Pit, A2 Ring, A3 Bowl, A4 Hall, A5 Tower, A6 Yard,
A7 Crossroads, A8 Bridge.

---

## Act 2

### Level 2-1

**New:** B04 flywheel auto (if 1-3 does not take it, see LEVEL-1-3.md 8),
F04 Blocker (10 health, shield wall, fires over it), and melee weapon 1
(fast, light, combo hits).

**Signature space:** a long workshop floor split by a 20 m conveyor gantry
at 3 m, so the player can go under it or across it. The Blockers form a
wall at the far end, and the lesson is flanking: the gantry puts the
player above and behind the shield line, and the shield can be carved
away by the auto blaster. The first 3 Blockers stand in a 12 m wide
arc so there is no straight line through them.

**Archetypes:** A4 Hall (workshop), A2 Ring (the gantry loop), A3 Bowl
(final room), A6 Yard (gauntlet).

**Sketch:** the signature hall, one character = 2 m.

```text
      +-------------------------+
      |  B   B   B    <- shield |
      |                         |
      |======= gantry ==========| 3 m up, 6 m wide
      |   R              R      |
      |                         |
      +-----------+-------------+
                  S
```

Proposed, Paul decides: a workshop floor (as above), a school hall with
a stage the gantry becomes, or a toy store's shelving aisle.

### Level 2-2

**New:** F06 Floater (35 health, floats, bouncing blobs, weak mouth), and
the ammo-for-kills tool (D9 vacuum: a weakened Foamer is stunned, E
extracts darts).

**Signature space:** A5 Tower, but inverted: the player starts at the top
and works down 15 m, and the Floaters rise to meet them. The tool is
taught on a landing with one stunned Slowmer and nothing else alive, so
the first extraction is free. The tower's 3 m floor gaps are wide enough
for a Floater and too wide to jump, so the descent is a chain of drops
and the way back up is a lift that only runs when the floor is clear.

**Archetypes:** A5 Tower (descent), A1 Pit (bottom), A7 Crossroads (mid),
A3 Bowl (gauntlet).

**Sketch:** tower, one character = 2 m.

```text
  +------------+
  | F    F     |  floor 3 (start)
  |____  ______|
  |    | |
  |  __|_|___  |  floor 2, 3 m gap
  |  |      |  |
  |  |______|__|  floor 1
  |     F      |
  +------------+
```

Proposed, Paul decides: a stairwell, a car park's ramps, or a lift shaft
in the Foam dimension.

### Level 2-3

**New:** B05 precision blaster (whistler darts, 5 health mag), F09 Crawler
(40 health, four legs, sustained pellet stream, destructible gun), and
destructible enemy guns as a general rule.

**Signature space:** A6 Yard with islands, but the islands are 3.5 m tall
and the Crawlers stand on top of four of them. The player's job is to
shoot the guns off, which needs height: the only way up is a 1.5 m
mantle chain on the two islands the Crawlers are not on. This is the
level that teaches "the weak point is on the thing that is shooting you"
by putting the weak points above the player's eye line.

**Archetypes:** A6 Yard (signature), A4 Hall (approach), A1 Pit (mid),
A2 Ring (gauntlet).

**Sketch:** the yard, one character = 2 m.

```text
   +----------------------+
   |  [C]      [C]        |   C = Crawler on a 3.5 m island
   |     [##]      [##]   |   ## = mantle chain up
   |        R      R      |
   |     [##]      [##]   |
   |  [C]      [C]        |
   +----------+-----------+
              S
```

Proposed, Paul decides: a warehouse roof, a playground with climbing
frames, or a foam plateau.

### Level 2-4

**New:** F07 Brawler (45 health, leaps and slams, shockwave ring you jump
over), melee weapon 2 (slow, big area). Mini-boss: a brawler pair.

**Signature space:** A1 Pit with a 1.2 m raised centre platform and no
rails, so the shockwave rings run out from the platform and the player
jumps them or steps down. Two Brawlers at once means two rings at
different rhythms: the fight is a jump-rope. The pit's ramps are the only
way out, and a Brawler that leaps to the top of a ramp closes them.

**Archetypes:** A1 Pit (signature), A8 Bridge (the drop in), A4 Hall
(approach), A7 Crossroads (gauntlet).

**Sketch:** the pit, one character = 2 m.

```text
   +---------------------+
   |   R           R     |
   |      +------+       |  platform 10 x 10 m, 1.2 m up
   |      |      |       |  ring runs out to the walls
   |      +------+       |
   |   R           R     |
   +----------+----------+
              S
```

Proposed, Paul decides: a gym floor with mats, a bouncy castle interior,
or a loading dock.

### Boss 2

**New:** nothing. Tests Act 2: sustained fire, height, weak points on
moving targets, and the vacuum under pressure.

**Signature space:** A7 Crossroads, 32 x 32 m, where the boss moves round
the four spokes and the player has to follow or cut it off. Health 500
(CONTENT-PLAN 3), three phases: a shielded phase that needs the melee
weapon to break, a phase where it flies to the spokes and needs B05, and
a last phase where it charges the hub and the answer is the vacuum on the
adds it spawns, to keep ammo up.

**Archetypes:** A7 Crossroads, with each spoke an A4 lane.

**Sketch:**

```text
        +--+   +--+
        |  | B |  |
   +----+  +---+  +----+
   |      hub 10 m      |
   |   +----------+     |
   |   |  pillar  |     |
   |   +----------+     |
   +----+  +---+  +----+
        |  | B |  |
        +--+   +--+
```

Proposed, Paul decides: the setting, and whether the boss is a promoted
Foamer (D5 colour variant) or its own rig.

## Act 3

### Level 3-1

**New:** B06 heavy (mega darts, 60 mag), F08 Trawler (80 health, arm
launchers as weak points, walls of foam balls with gaps).

**Signature space:** A4 Hall, 24 m wide and 60 m long, with three 8 m
arches. The Trawlers walk down it and fill the arches with ball walls, so
the player reads the gaps and moves between arches. This is the widest
hall in the game and the only place where a 60-round mag makes sense.
Setting: [Paul] asks whether Act 3 is in the Foam dimension.

**Archetypes:** A4 Hall (signature), A2 Ring, A3 Bowl, A6 Yard (gauntlet).

**Sketch:**

```text
  +------------------------------+
  |  ###   ###   ###             |  arches 8 m wide
  |   T      T      T            |  Trawlers walk toward the player
  |  ###   ###   ###             |
  +--------------+---------------+
                 S
```

### Level 3-2

**New:** B07 launcher (mega darts, arcing, area blast, remote detonate),
F10 Turbo (30 health, flying, homing shots, jets as weak point), melee
weapon 3 (charged slam).

**Signature space:** A5 Tower, 20 x 20 m, 5 floors, 25 m tall, open in the
middle: a shaft. The Turbos circle the shaft and the player fights
upward with an arcing launcher, which is the only blaster whose arc can
reach a target above and behind cover. The slam knocks a Turbo out of the
air, which is the melee's teaching moment at the top floor.

**Archetypes:** A5 Tower (signature), A8 Bridge, A1 Pit, A6 Yard.

**Sketch:**

```text
   +----------+
   |  T    T  |  floor 4
   |  |    |  |
   |  +----+  |  25 m shaft, open middle
   |  T    T  |
   |  |    |  |
   +-----+----+
         S
```

### Level 3-3

**New:** F11 Seawall (60 health, shield only a parry breaks, combo then a
parryable finisher), F12 Ambusher (25 health, hides in foam growths, leaps
out). No new blaster.

**Signature space:** A6 Yard where **every island is a foam growth**: six
3.2 m cubes of growth, five of which hide an Ambusher. The player has to
clear growths to move safely, and the Seawalls make clearing slow because
they must be parried first. This is the level where destruction is
defence, not just route.

**Archetypes:** A6 Yard (signature), A2 Ring, A7 Crossroads, A3 Bowl.

**Sketch:**

```text
   +----------------------+
   |  [G]      [G]        |   G = growth, 5 hide an Ambusher
   |     [G]      [G]     |
   |  [G]      [G]        |
   |        W             |   W = Seawall, parry to break the shield
   +----------+-----------+
              S
```

### Level 3-4

**New:** B08 gatling (foam balls, spin-up, slows movement), F13
Biomechanic (25 health, rebuilds other Foamers, tank weak point).
Everything at once.

**Signature space:** A7 Crossroads with four spokes that each hold a
different combination from the whole game: a shield wall, a Trawler, a
pair of Turbos, and a growth patch of Ambushers. The hub is the only
safe ground, and the Biomechanic stands in it, rebuilding whatever the
player kills in a spoke. The player has to choose the order, which is the
act's thesis: 12 levels of vocabulary, one open choice.

**Archetypes:** A7 Crossroads (signature), each spoke reusing A4, A6,
A3 and A2.

**Sketch:**

```text
        +--+   +--+
        |wall  flyers|
   +----+  +---+  +----+
   |      | M |         |   M = Biomechanic in the hub
   |      +---+         |
   |   growth    tank   |
   +----+  +---+  +----+
        |  |   |  |
        +--+   +--+
```

### Boss 3

**New:** nothing. The finale. Health 800, three phases that reuse the
whole kit, and a space that changes between phases.

**Signature space:** A1 Pit inside A5 Tower: the arena is a 30 m wide pit
at the bottom of a 40 m shaft, and the boss climbs the walls between
phases. Phase 1 is the pit floor, phase 2 is the ring ledge 8 m up, phase
3 is the shaft itself, where the player rides a rising platform and the
boss attacks from the walls. The platform is the only footing, 6 x 6 m,
and it rises 2 m every 10 s, so the fight is a climb.

**Archetypes:** A1 Pit plus A5 Tower stacked.

**Sketch:**

```text
   +------------------+
   |  B               |  shaft 40 m, walls climbable by the boss
   |                  |
   |   +----------+   |  platform 6 x 6, rises 2 m / 10 s
   |   |  player  |   |
   |   +----------+   |
   |  B               |
   +--------+---------+
            S
```

Proposed, Paul decides: the boss, the setting, and whether the rising
platform is a lift, a foam column or something the boss makes.
