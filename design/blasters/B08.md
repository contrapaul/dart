in `design/CONTENT-PLAN.md` section 4 and his reference photos
`design/referenceimages/B08reference.webp`, `B08reference2.webp` and

## The restated brief (Paul, 2026-11-10)

Verbatim, because it is what the model was built to: "no 'hopper' atop the
barrel, this is a side magazine fed blaster. The handle is over the body of
the blaster. The mod which adds a 2nd barrel adds the entire 6 barrel
assembly, and both would spin on individual axis when fired".

The 2026-11-08 build is gone. This is a different blaster: six exposed
barrels a minigun turns, fed from a stick magazine on the side, carried by
a handle over the body.

## Design brief

**What the photos show (for reference only):** a big foam gatling about
80 cm long. Six long exposed tubes in a ring around a hollow centre, held
by three thin bands, at the front; a round drive housing behind them; a
long boxy rear body with moulded panels; a tall arched rear handle that
rises from the back of the body with the trigger under its top end; a
second upright handle on top of the drive housing; a flip-up feed cover on
top; a long belt of darts hanging under and around the body. Printed brand
text, a model number and a logo plate on the side. Lime body, purple
handles and bands, orange tubes.

**What B08 is:** a heavy hip-fired gatling that sprays foam balls. Built
around a spinning six-barrel cluster in front, a boxy body with a motor
block and cooling ribs behind it, a side magazine, and one handle over the
body that the hand holds.

### Originality list (all required)

1. **Six exposed barrels round a hub** with a drive plate behind and a
   retaining plate in front, so the ring shows turning, not a shroud. A
   yellow band near each muzzle and a plain orange bumper ring at the
   front.
2. **A boxy body with a rounded motor block** at the back and a row of
   cooling ribs on each side, instead of the photos' long panelled body.
3. **A side magazine on the right**, a stick of foam balls tilted back a
   few degrees, with yellow feed lips, follower ridges and a base plate.
   No belt, no flip-up cover, no hopper.
4. **One carry handle over the body**, two posts and a crossbar with
   flared palm swells, the trigger and its guard under the front of it.
   Not the photos' tall arched rear handle plus upright top handle.
5. **Two clusters for the Twin mod**: the second is a complete second
   six-barrel assembly, its own drive plate, retaining plate and bumper,
   bolted to the left on a saddle plate and a strut, and it turns on its
   own axis.
6. **No text, logos, decals or printed patterns.**
7. **Our colours,** not lime, purple and orange: those three are the
   Foamers' palette, so a blaster in them would read as an enemy.

### Colours

Toy plastic, slightly glossy (roughness 0.4). Taken so far: B01 blue, B02
yellow, B03 purple, B04 lime, B05 deep red, B06 teal, B07 navy with red.

- Body, motor block, carry handle: **white** (`#f2f2ee`).
- Barrels, hub, plates, magazine body, grips, ribs, saddle and strut:
  **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Accents (barrel bands, feed lips, magazine ridges, base plate, trigger,
  side stripes): **foam yellow** (`#ffd23f`).
- Muzzle bumpers: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`).

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Spin-up: the cluster winds up over about 0.8 s and stays fast while
  firing, winds down over about 1.2 s. Twin turns `barrels_2` on its own
  axis too. The game spins both; `wheelSpin` in `data/blasters.json` is 60
  rev/s and is a number to tune on the range.
- Fire: a fast small shake back and a steady climb.
- Reload: the magazine drops out and a fresh one goes in (3.0 s).
