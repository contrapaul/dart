## Design brief

**What the photos show (for reference only):** a big foam gatling about
80 cm long. Six long exposed tubes in a ring around a hollow centre,
held by three thin bands, at the front; a round drive housing behind
them; a long boxy rear body with moulded panels; a tall arched rear
handle that rises from the back of the body with the trigger under its
top end; a second upright handle on top of the drive housing; a flip-up
feed cover on top; a long belt of darts hanging under and around the
body. Printed brand text, a model number and a logo plate on the side.
Lime body, purple handles and bands, orange tubes.

**What B08 must be:** a heavy hip-fired gatling that sprays foam balls.
It keeps the photos' big idea (a spinning barrel cluster in front of a
chunky body, carried with two hands) but becomes a different blaster,
built around **a ball hopper on top and a shrouded barrel cluster**,
since it fires balls, not darts.

### Originality list (all required)

1. **A round ball hopper on top** of the body, see-through so the balls
   show, with a hinged lid, instead of the photos' dart belt and feed
   cover.
2. **A shrouded barrel cluster:** six short barrels inside one fat round
   shroud with four large oval windows, so the barrels show turning
   through them, and a plain round orange bumper at the front, instead of
   six long exposed tubes on three bands with a hollow centre.
3. **A pistol grip under the rear of the body** and **a vertical front
   grip** under the shroud, instead of the tall arched rear handle and
   the upright top handle.
4. **A rounded motor block** at the back with a row of cooling ribs,
   instead of the long boxy panelled body.
5. **No belt, no darts, no flip-up cover.**
6. **No text, logos, decals or printed patterns.**
7. **Our colours,** not lime, purple and orange: those three are the
   Foamers' palette, so a blaster in them would read as an enemy.

### Size and parts (centimetres, true size)

Overall about 74 cm long (motor block back to the bumper), 30 cm tall
(hopper top to the pistol grip bottom), 14 cm across the shroud.
Muzzle points forward.

Origin at the pistol grip, where the trigger hand holds it.

### Colours

Toy plastic, slightly glossy (Paul's call, see the question). Taken so
far: B01 blue, B02 yellow, B03 purple, B04 lime, B05 deep red, B06 teal,
B07 navy with red. A proposal:

- Body, motor block, shroud, hopper lid: **white** (`#f2f2ee`).
- Barrels, grips, ribs: **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Accents (trigger, shroud end rings, window rims): **foam yellow**
  (`#ffd23f`).
- Muzzle bumper: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`).
- Hopper: see-through **smoke** (about 50% clear), the balls inside
  **foam blue** (`#2ec4ff`, the player's dart colour).

### Renders

The four turnaround views, plus the barrel cluster from the front, plus
the Twin mod on.

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Spin-up: the barrel cluster winds up over about 0.8 s and spins fast
  while firing, winds down over about 1.2 s.
- Fire: a fast small shake back and a steady climb.
- Reload: the hopper lid swings up, balls pour in, the lid snaps shut
  (3.0 s).
