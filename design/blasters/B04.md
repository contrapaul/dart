## Design brief

**What the photo shows (for reference only):** a long tactical-style foam
rifle about 75 cm long: blue and silver body with grey panels and a printed
pattern, a tube stock with an adjustable pad and a round button, a tall
carry-handle scope at the back of the rail, a long top rail with cut-out
slots, a vertical front grip under the handguard, a straight half-length
clear magazine in front of the trigger, a separate pistol grip, and an
orange slotted muzzle device. Printed text and a logo on the side.

**What B04 must be:** a fast, aggressive flywheel blaster. It keeps the
photo's long rifle layout (stock, pistol grip, magazine ahead of the
trigger, handguard, front grip) but becomes a different blaster, built
around a **flywheel housing**. The flywheels are internal: the housing is
closed and the wheels are never seen (Paul, 2026-11-02).

### Originality list (all required)

1. **A flywheel housing** above the magazine well: a rounded, closed
   bulge on both sides (the photo has none). No window: the wheels are
   inside and never seen.
2. **A curved magazine** (a gentle banana curve) instead of the straight
   half-length clip.
3. **A solid fixed stock** with one oval cut-out and a rounded rubber pad,
   instead of the adjustable tube stock with a button.
4. **No tall scope:** a low notch rear sight and a low post front sight on
   the rail.
5. **An angled foregrip** (raked forward about 30 degrees) instead of a
   vertical grip.
6. **A short barrel with a round, chunky muzzle bumper** (a ring with
   three shallow grooves), not a slotted muzzle device.
7. **No text, logos, decals or printed patterns.**
8. **Our colours** (below), not the photo's blue, silver and grey.

### Colours

Toy plastic, slightly glossy, flexible (Paul):

- Body (receiver, housing, handguard): bright **foam lime** (`#b6ff3b`
  family).
- Furniture (grip, foregrip, stock, rail): **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Accents (trigger, sights): **foam purple**
  (`#9b5cff`).
- Flywheels: **foam yellow** (`#ffd23f`), hidden inside the housing.
- Muzzle bumper: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`).
- Magazine: see-through **orange** (about 50% clear), blue darts with
  orange tips inside.

The four turnaround views, plus side close-ups of the **trigger area**

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Fire: a small kick back per shot (about 1 cm) and a climb that grows
  with the burst (the recoil above), the flywheels spinning fast.
- Spin-up: the wheels speed up over about 0.2 s when the trigger is
  pulled and slow over about 1 s after release.
- Reload: the magazine drops down and forward, a full one rises in; the
  blaster tilts a little to the left while this happens (1.8 s).
