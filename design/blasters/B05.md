## Design brief

**What the photo shows (for reference only):** a long foam carbine about
95 cm long. A short receiver with a printed film-franchise pattern and
logo on the side, a ring red-dot sight and a flip-up rear sight on a top
rail, a round charging knob on the side, a straight stick magazine ahead
of a separate pistol grip, a skeleton stock with a see-through cylinder
in it, a long bare thin barrel, and a large forked muzzle device with
prongs.

**What B05 must be:** a patient, precise bolt-action blaster with a
scope. It keeps the photo's long, lean silhouette (a short body, a long
barrel line, a stock) but becomes a different blaster, built around a
**bolt and a tube scope**.

### Originality list (all required)

1. **A bolt handle** on the right side, a bent lever with a ball knob,
   that lifts and slides back; the photo has a round knob only.
2. **A tube scope** on two rings, with a rubber eye cup, instead of the
   ring red-dot and the flip-up sight.
3. **A full-length fluted handguard** around the barrel (long grooves
   along it), instead of a bare thin barrel.
4. **A plain round muzzle bumper** with one ring groove, instead of the
   forked prong device.
5. **A short 5-dart box magazine**, nearly flush with the body, instead
   of the long stick magazine.
6. **A solid thumbhole stock** with a cheek rest and a rubber pad,
   instead of the skeleton stock and its see-through cylinder.
7. **No text, logos, decals or printed patterns.**
8. **Our colours** (below), not the photo's yellow and orange print.

### Size and parts (centimetres, true size)

Overall about 92 cm long (stock pad to muzzle), 22 cm tall (scope top to
magazine bottom), 6 cm wide at the receiver (10 cm at the bolt knob).
Muzzle points forward.

| Part | Node | Size (L x H x W) | Shape, and how it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receiver | `body` | 24 x 7 x 5.5 | Rounded slab; the bolt runs along its right side, the magazine under its front |
| Handguard | `body` | 40 x 5 dia | Round, with 6 long grooves along it, from the receiver forward |
| Barrel tip | `body` | 3 x 2.4 dia | Shows past the handguard |
| Muzzle bumper | `body` | 3 x 3.6 dia | Plain round ring with one groove, orange (the toy safety tip) |
| Scope | `body` | 30 x 4 dia | A tube on two rings over the receiver, a wider front bell (5 cm) and a rubber eye cup at the back; the lens faces are flat discs |
| Bolt | `bolt` | 6 x 1.5 x 4 | A lever from the receiver's right side, bent down at the end, with a 2 cm ball knob; rotates up about its own local Z (along the bore) and then slides back along local Z. Origin where it enters the receiver |
| Pistol grip | `body` | 10 x 4.5 x 3 | **Simple and comfortable to hold**, as B01's: one smooth rounded piece, angled back about 15 degrees, grip texture a small tiling normal map only |
| Trigger guard | `body` | loop | Opening about 4.5 cm long and 2.5 cm tall, in front of the grip |

Origin at the pistol grip, where the trigger hand holds it.

### Colours

Toy plastic, slightly glossy, flexible (Paul). B01 is blue, B02 yellow,
B03 purple, B04 lime:

- Receiver, handguard, stock: **foam white** (`#fff6e8`).
- Scope, grip, rings, bolt lever: **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Accents (bolt knob, trigger, the handguard grooves' inner faces):
  **foam blue** (`#2ec4ff`).
- Muzzle bumper: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`).
- Magazine: see-through **blue** (about 50% clear).
- Scope lenses: dark blue-black, slightly glossy.

### Renders

The four turnaround views, plus a close-up of the bolt from the right and
of the scope's eye cup from behind.

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Fire: a short sharp kick back (about 3 cm) and a small climb.
- Bolt: after each shot the knob lifts (about 60 degrees about the bore),
  slides back 5 cm, forward again and down (0.6 s in all).
- Zoom: the view narrows; the scope stays in place on the model.
- Reload: the magazine drops, a full one goes in, then the bolt is worked
  (1.8 s).
