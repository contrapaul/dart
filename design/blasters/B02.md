## Design brief

**What the photo shows (for reference only):** a chunky toy pistol about
30 cm long. A cocking hammer at the back of the top, a full-length grey
top rail, a big orange front block holding four exposed darts in a 2 by
2 cluster, an open frame loop that runs under the barrels and forward
past the muzzle, a separate trigger guard, and a grip wrapped like a
bandage with a lanyard ring at the bottom. A brand name, a model name
and a printed emblem on the side.

**What B02 must be:** a compact, quick semi-auto sidearm that is never
empty. It keeps the photo's chunky toy pistol feel (a short heavy front,
a big hand-filling grip, toy-plastic panels) but becomes a different
blaster: magazine-fed from the grip, with a slide that kicks on every
shot.

### Originality list (all required)

1. **A single barrel** with a round, flared muzzle bumper (a small bell,
   a nod to the name), instead of four exposed darts in a 2 by 2 block.
2. **A magazine in the grip** that drops out of the bottom on reload;
   the photo loads from the front.
3. **A slide** on top that kicks back on each shot, instead of a cocking
   hammer.
4. **No frame loop** under the barrel: a plain rounded trigger guard and
   an open front.
5. **A low notch rear sight and a post front sight** on the slide,
   instead of a full-length top rail.
6. **A smooth grip** to B01's comfort rules, with no wrap, bandage or
   lanyard ring.
7. **No text, logos, decals, emblems or printed patterns.**
8. **Our colours** (below), not the photo's orange, grey and brown.

### Size and parts (centimetres, true size)

Overall about 28 cm long (back of the slide to the muzzle), 17 cm tall
(top of the slide to the bottom of the grip), 4 cm wide. Muzzle points
forward.

| Part | Node | Size (L x H x W) | Shape, and how it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame | `body` | 22 x 4 x 3.6 | Rounded slab under the slide; the barrel sits in its front |
| Barrel | `body` | 4 x 2.2 dia | Sticks out of the frame's front, under the slide's nose |
| Muzzle bumper | `body` | 2.5 x 3.4 dia | A small flared bell on the barrel tip, orange (the toy safety tip) |
| Slide | `slide` | 20 x 4 x 3.4 | On top of the frame, with a few rounded grip grooves at the back; moves back along its own local Z on each shot. Origin at its rest centre |
| Sights | `slide` (child) | small | Low notch at the back, post at the front, both on the slide |
| Grip | `body` | 11 x 4.5 x 3.2 | **Simple and comfortable to hold**, as B01's: one smooth rounded piece, angled back about 15 degrees, a slight palm swell, a small flare at the bottom; grip texture is a small tiling normal map only, never modelled ribs. Hollow at the bottom for the magazine |
| Trigger guard | `body` | loop | Rounded; opening about 4 cm long and 2.5 cm tall, in front of the grip |
| Trigger | `trigger` | 2.2 x 1.2 x 0.8 | A blade on a pivot at its top near the back of the guard opening; the finger rests on its concave front face. A positive rotation about local X swings the lower end back toward the grip (as B01) |
| Magazine | `mag` | 9 x 3 x 2.2 | A short box inside the grip, its base plate flush with the grip's bottom; see-through, the top dart visible through a slot in the grip's side; drops straight down on reload. Origin at its rest centre |
| Muzzle | `muzzle_1` | empty | At the bumper's front centre |

Mods: no geometry for Burst. Heavy rounds may show a thicker barrel
sleeve, `mod_heavy`, hidden by default (a question for Paul below).

Origin at the grip, where the trigger hand holds it.

### Colours

Toy plastic, slightly glossy, flexible (Paul). B01 is blue, B03 purple,
B04 lime, so B02 takes a lead colour of its own:

- Slide and frame: bright **foam yellow** (`#ffd23f` family).
- Grip, trigger guard: **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Accents (trigger, sights, slide grooves): **foam purple**
  (`#9b5cff`).
- Muzzle bumper: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`).
- Magazine: see-through **lime** (about 50% clear), a blue dart with an
  orange tip showing at the top.

### Renders

The four turnaround views, plus a side close-up of the trigger and grip
with the magazine half dropped.

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Fire: the slide snaps back about 2.5 cm and returns within 0.1 s, the
  whole blaster kicks back about 1.5 cm and tips up a little.
- Burst (mod 1): three slide kicks in quick succession.
- Heavy (mod 2): a bigger kick and a slower slide return.
- Reload: the magazine drops straight down out of the grip and fades, a
  full one rises in, the blaster tilts a little to the right (1.0 s).
