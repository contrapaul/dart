## Design brief

**What the photo shows (for reference only):** a chunky foam launcher
about 70 cm long. A big revolving cylinder in the middle with six large
darts showing at its front, a long body with a pump, a vertical foregrip
hanging under the barrel, a thin top rail ending in a rod-like rear
handle, an orange barrel shroud and sight at the front, a pistol grip
with a white insert, printed brand text and a logo on the sides.

**What B07 must be:** a heavy pump launcher that lobs explosive rounds.
It keeps the photo's chunky, front-heavy launcher feel (a fat barrel, a
pump, a pistol grip) but becomes a different blaster, built around a
**tube magazine under a wide launch tube**.

### Originality list (all required)

1. **A tube magazine** under the launch tube, holding five rounds in a
   line (their warheads seen through a long slot), instead of the
   revolving cylinder.
2. **A wide launch tube** with a plain round bumper, instead of a narrow
   barrel inside an orange shroud.
3. **A ribbed pump sleeve** around the tube magazine, worked by the
   support hand, instead of a vertical foregrip.
4. **A folding ladder sight** on top for the arc (a frame with three
   rungs), instead of the thin rail and the rod handle.
5. **A short solid stock** with a rubber pad (the photo has none).
6. **A plain smooth grip** to B01's comfort rules, no white insert.
7. **No text, logos, decals or printed patterns.**
8. **Paul's colours:** navy blue with red accents.

### Size and parts (centimetres, true size)

Overall about 72 cm long (stock pad to muzzle), 22 cm tall (ladder sight
top to grip bottom), 8 cm wide at the launch tube. Muzzle points forward.

| Part | Node | Size (L x H x W) | Shape, and how it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receiver | `body` | 24 x 9 x 6.5 | Rounded slab; the launch tube leaves its front, the tube magazine runs under its front |
| Launch tube | `body` | 34 x 8 dia | Wide and round, two raised red bands |

| Pump sleeve | `pump` | 12 x 6.5 dia | Around the front of the tube magazine, with red ribs; slides back and forward along local Z. Origin at its rest centre |
| Pistol grip | `body` | 10 x 4.5 x 3.2 | **Simple and comfortable to hold**, as B01's |

No mod geometry: both mods change the round, not the blaster.

Origin at the pistol grip, where the trigger hand holds it.

### Colours

Toy plastic, slightly glossy (Paul: navy blue with red accents):

- Receiver, launch tube, tube magazine, stock: **navy blue** (`#1f2f5c`).
- Accents (tube bands, pump ribs, ladder rungs, trigger, rear sight):
  **red** (`#d13438`).
- Grip, pump sleeve body, stock pad: **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Muzzle bumper: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`).
- Round warheads in the slot: **foam yellow** (`#ffd23f`).

### Renders

The four turnaround views, plus a close-up of the tube magazine's slot

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Fire: a heavy kick back (about 4 cm) and a climb.
- Pump: the sleeve slides back 7 cm and forward after each shot.
- Reload: the tube magazine drops down and forward, a full one goes in
  (2.5 s).
