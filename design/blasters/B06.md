## Design brief

**What the photo shows (for reference only):** a big foam light machine
gun about 85 cm long. A long round barrel shroud with rows of oval
slots, a front sight on top and a top rail, a folding bipod with long
ribbed legs under the front, a large square see-through box magazine
under the receiver, a side window showing a spring, two short dart tubes
on the side, a tall slotted charging lever on top at the back, a
separate pistol grip, and a skeleton stock of two rods. Printed brand and
model names on the side.

**What B06 must be:** a heavy, steady machine-gun style blaster firing
mega darts. It keeps the photo's big, front-heavy silhouette (a long
thick shroud, a feed under the middle, a stock) but becomes a different
blaster, built around a **chunky house-shaped magazine**.

### Originality list (all required)

1. **A chunky house-shaped magazine** (Paul) under the receiver: seen
   side-on it is a house, square walls with a roof whose peak feeds up
   into the receiver, instead of the photo's stepped square box.
2. **A carry handle** on top of the receiver, instead of the bipod (no
   bipod at all).
3. **A thick shroud with a row of round vents** and three raised rings
   around it, instead of rows of oval slots.
4. **A vertical front grip** under the shroud for the support hand.
5. **A solid stock** with one long cut-out and a rubber pad, instead of
   the two-rod skeleton stock.
6. **No side window, no side dart tubes, no tall charging lever**: a
   small cocking handle on the right side instead.
7. **No text, logos, decals or printed patterns.**
8. **Our accents** (below): the teal lead is Paul's choice, like the
   reference, but the accents, the magazine and the bumper are ours.

top to magazine bottom), 7 cm wide at the receiver, 9 cm across the

### Colours

Toy plastic, slightly glossy, flexible (Paul). B01 is blue, B02 yellow,
B03 purple, B04 lime, B05 white:

- Receiver, shroud, carry handle: **teal** (`#2fbfc8`, Paul: like the
  reference).
- Stock, grips, rings: **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Accents (trigger, sights, cocking knob, the vents' inner faces):
  **foam yellow** (`#ffd23f`).
- Muzzle bumper: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`).
- Magazine: see-through **lime** (about 50% clear), charcoal mega darts
  with orange tips inside.

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Fire: a heavy kick back (about 2.5 cm) on each shot and a slow climb.
- Reload: the cocking handle is pulled back, the magazine drops off and a
  full one goes on, the handle is let go (2.4 s).
