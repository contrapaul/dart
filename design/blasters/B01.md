## Design brief

**What the photo shows (for reference only):** a compact, angular,
magazine-fed foam blaster about 55 cm long. A purple slab receiver with a
patterned decal panel on the side, a ribbed black top rail with a tall
hooded rear sight, a slotted barrel shroud with a short rail on top, a
long thin orange barrel sticking out of the front, a black angled pistol
grip with an orange trigger, and a long translucent orange stick magazine
ahead of the grip. No stock. It reads as a toy compact rifle.

**What B01 must be:** a pump shotgun-style starter that fires three darts
at once. It keeps the photo's chunky toy-plastic feel (slab receiver,
ribbed rail, vented shroud, see-through magazine) but becomes a different
blaster.

### Originality list (all required)

1. **Three short barrels** in a triangle (two below, one above) instead of
   one long thin barrel.
2. **A pump forend** under the shroud that slides back and forth (the
   photo has none).
3. **A short skeletal stock** at the back (the photo has none).
4. **Round vent holes** in the shroud instead of long slots.
5. **A small ring sight** on the rail instead of the tall hooded block.
6. **No text, logos, decals or patterns.** The side panel is plain with
   two raised ribs.
7. **Our colours** (below), not the photo's purple and orange.

magazine bottom), 6 cm wide at the receiver. Muzzle points forward.

### Colours

From `generalcolorreference.webp`, toy plastic, slightly glossy:

- Body (receiver, shroud): bright **foam blue** (`#2ec4ff` family).
- Furniture (grip, rail, stock, pump ribs): **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Accents (trigger, ring sight, side ribs): **foam yellow** (`#ffd23f`).
- Barrel tips: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`), the toy safety tip.
- Magazine: see-through **pink** (`#ff4fa3`, about 50% clear), darts inside
  in blue with orange tips (as the game's standard darts).

These are placeholders for Paul's eye; colours are flexible (Paul).

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Fire: the whole blaster kicks back about 4 cm and tilts up about 5
  degrees, then springs back. The trigger rotates back and returns.
- Pump: the forend slides back 6 cm and forward again after each shot
  (0.45 s).
- Reload: the magazine drops about 15 cm and fades, a full one rises in;
  the blaster dips while this happens (1.4 s).
