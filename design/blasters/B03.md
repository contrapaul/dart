## Design brief

**What the photo shows (for reference only):** a long lever-action foam
rifle about 80 cm long: a white skeleton stock with a patterned pad, a
blue receiver with a printed name, a big lever loop under the action, a
round wheel on the side, a long orange barrel and tube, a rail on top, and
a separate orange "accuracy booster" attachment. Printed text on the side.

**What B03 must be:** a chunky break-action double-barrel shotgun. It
keeps the photo's long, classic shoulder-blaster shape and its bright
two-tone plastic, but is a different blaster: two fat barrels that hinge
down to reload.

### Originality list (all required)

1. **Two fat barrels side by side** that hinge down at a pin (break
   action), instead of a single barrel and a tube.
2. **No lever loop:** a normal trigger guard under the action.
3. **No side wheel:** a plain receiver side with a raised oval panel.
4. **A top latch** (a short lever on top of the action) that swings
   sideways to open the barrels.
5. **A solid stock** with a pistol-grip wrist and a rounded rubber pad,
   not a skeleton stock.
6. **A chunky fore-end** under the barrels instead of a rail and tube.
7. **No text, logos, decals or printed patterns.**
8. **Our colours** (below), not the photo's white, blue and orange.

### Size and parts (centimetres, true size)

Overall about 80 cm long (stock pad to muzzles), 20 cm tall (latch top to
stock bottom), 7 cm wide across the barrels. Muzzles point forward.

| Part | Node | Size (L x H x W) | Shape, and how it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Action (receiver) | `body` | 14 x 7 x 5.5 | Short rounded block the barrels hinge from; raised oval panel on each side. The hinge pin sits at its lower front corner |
| Stock | `body` | 34 x 14 x 4.5 | Solid, rounded, with a **pistol-grip wrist** behind the trigger guard (the trigger hand holds the wrist) and a rounded rubber butt pad. The wrist is **simple and comfortable**: smooth, rounded on every edge a hand touches, about 4.5 cm deep and 3.5 cm wide; grip texture is a small tiling normal map on its own material only, never modelled ribs |
| Trigger guard | `body` | loop | Under the action, joining the wrist to the action; opening about 5 cm long and 2.5 cm tall, fully in front of the wrist |
| Trigger | `trigger` | 2.5 x 1.2 x 0.8 | One flat blade hanging from a pivot at its top near the **back** of the guard opening, about 1 to 1.5 cm in front of the wrist. The finger rests on its **front** face, so the front face curves concave forward and the tip curls slightly forward; the finger space is in front of it. A positive rotation about local X swings the lower end back toward the wrist (as B01). Each pull fires one barrel |
| Barrels | `breach` | 42 x 3.4 dia each | Two fat tubes side by side, joined along their length by a flat top rib with a small bead front sight. They hinge down to reload: **origin on the hinge pin** (the action's lower front corner), opening by rotating about local X so the muzzles drop about 40 degrees and the chamber ends rise out of the action. Everything that moves with the barrels is a child of `breach` |
| Fore-end | `breach` (child) | 16 x 4 x 6 | Chunky rounded grip under the barrels for the support hand, with the same grip texture; it opens with the barrels |
| Muzzle tips | `breach` (child) | 2 x 3.8 dia each | Fat orange rings on the barrel ends (the toy safety tip) |
| Shells | `shell_1`, `shell_2` (children of `breach`) | 7 x 3 dia each | Foam shells sitting in the chambers, visible only when the barrels are open: a chunky see-through tube with a solid base, the tips of a cluster of darts showing at the top. Origin at each shell's centre so they can fly out on reload |
| Top latch | `latch` | 5 x 1.2 x 2 | Short lever on top of the action, behind the barrels; pushed sideways by the thumb to open. Origin at its pivot (a vertical pin at its front end); opening rotates it about local Y about 30 degrees to the right |
| Muzzles | `muzzle_1`, `muzzle_2` (children of `breach`) | empties | At the centre of each barrel tip |
| Quad mod | `mod_quad` (child of `breach`) | | A second pair of barrels below the first (over and under), with its own two shells `shell_3`, `shell_4` and muzzles `muzzle_3`, `muzzle_4`; hidden by default |

Origin of the whole model at the stock's wrist, where the trigger hand
holds it.

### Colours

Toy plastic, slightly glossy, flexible (Paul):

- Barrels and action: bright **foam purple** (`#9b5cff` family).
- Stock and fore-end: **charcoal** (`#2b2b33`).
- Accents (latch, trigger, bead sight, the oval side panels): **foam
  yellow** (`#ffd23f`).
- Muzzle tips: **orange** (`#ff7a1a`).
- Shells: see-through **pink** (about 50% clear) with a yellow base, blue
  dart tips with orange ends showing at the top.

### Renders

The four turnaround views, plus: a side close-up of the **trigger area**,
and a side and a three-quarter view with the **barrels open** (breach
rotated 40 degrees, latch turned, shells visible), to check the hinge and
that nothing passes through the action.

### Motion (for ART-05)

- Fire: a heavy kick back (about 6 cm) and up (about 8 degrees), slow
  return; the trigger pulls and returns.
- Reload: the latch turns, the barrels drop open, the shells pop up and
  out, two new shells drop in, the barrels snap shut, the latch returns
  (1.6 s).
