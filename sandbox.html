Foamer sandbox

Greybox stand-ins for each roster role. Shapes, sizes and movement are for discussion; names, looks and colours are Paul's. Grey is foam, dark grey is gear and weapons, orange marks muzzles and throwables, yellow is the foam core.

    Click a Foamer to shoot it with:

    Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. The tall clear figure is you (1.8 m). Dart: 1 damage, small wound. Heavy: 5, big wound. Blast: 30, pops it if that kills it in one big hit. A part falls off when its own health runs out (a share of the Foamer's) or it loses 40% of its foam; losing the head and an arm downs it.
