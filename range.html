Mods

Keys 1 to 8 or the mouse wheel change the blaster. Esc pauses and shows this panel; click the canvas or press Play to shoot again. T respawns the line. The time-to-kill panel is the small box in the lower left.

The graph shows one bar per frame: its height is the time since the last frame, the orange part is the game's own work. A hitch is a frame much longer than the display's refresh, or one that ran the wrong number of physics steps. Long: the game worked too long. Pacing: the frame came late while the game was idle (frame timing, or a browser pause such as garbage collection). Back-quote hides the graph.
