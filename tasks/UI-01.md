## Stage 1: health, damage, HUD

- **`src/player/health.ts`:** `PlayerHealth` with `hp`, `max` (100, from
  `data/player.json` `health.max`), `damage(amount, from?)`,
  `heal(amount)`, `dead`, and a short invulnerability after each hit
  (`health.hitGrace`, 0.5 s). `damage` multiplies by the difficulty's
  damage taken (stage 2 sets it; 1 until then). Pure, no DOM.
- **Damage:** each attack in `data/foamers.json` gains `"damage"` (F01
  bonk 12, headbutt 8; this is the only change to that file). The pages'
  `foamers.onBonk` calls `health.damage`. Keep the existing behaviour of
  the onBonk chain (the sounds wrap it).
- **Popped (death):** at 0 hp the game pauses into a "You popped!"
  overlay with a **Try again** button: it respawns the player at the
  spawn with full health and clears the Foamers (the range keeps its own
  respawn rules). No gore, no red: a foam burst colour (`foam-core`) and
  a toy-like tone.
- **HUD v1** (`src/ui/hud.ts`, DOM): chunky, toy-like, system fonts only.
  - Health bottom-left: a rounded bar in foam lime that drains, the
    number beside it, a quick white flash of the bar on each hit.
  - Ammo bottom-right as now, restyled to match (same data, same
    no-churn updates).
  - A hit flash: a brief edge glow in cream (`#fff6e8`) on taking damage,
    never red; it respects the "reduce flashes" setting (stage 2).
- Both pages: the HUD shows health on the main page; the range shows it
  too and gets a **God mode** switch (no damage) in its panel.

**Tests (`tests/player/health.test.ts`):** damage lowers hp; the grace
blocks a second hit within 0.5 s and allows one after; hp never goes
below 0 or over max; `dead` at 0; the damage-taken multiplier applies.

Commit: `UI-01 stage 1: player health, damage and HUD v1`.

## Stage 2: pause menu and settings

- **Pause menu** (Esc or gamepad Start, as now, on the main page): a
  panel over the dimmed game with **Resume**, **Settings** and
  **Restart** (as Try again). Click Resume or the canvas to play.
- **Settings panel** (from the pause menu, and from the title overlay
  before the first click):
  - Difficulty: Story, Normal, Hard, Foam Fury (D10; Normal default).
    Each preset sets the sliders below that it covers; moving a slider
    after picking a preset shows "Custom".
  - Damage taken (25% to 200%).
  - Mouse sensitivity, invert look, field of view (60 to 100, default
    75).
  - Volume (master) and mute (the engine's `volume` and `muted`).
  - Reduce screen shake, reduce flashes, reduce head bob (switches; head
    bob and shake are read by later tasks, store them now).
- **`data/difficulty.json`:** the four presets: damage taken (Story 0.5,
  Normal 1, Hard 1.5, Foam Fury 2) and a `director` key per preset
  (`story`, `normal`, `hard`, `fury`) that ENG-08's director will read.
  Names stay as in D10.
- **`src/core/settings.ts`:** one `Settings` object, loaded from and
  saved to `localStorage` (`dart.settings`, versioned, with defaults for
  missing keys; wrapped in try/catch so a blocked storage still plays),
  with a change callback the pages use to apply sensitivity, invert, FOV,
  volume and damage taken live.
- Keyboard and mouse only for the menus in this task; Tab order works.

**Tests (`tests/core/settings.test.ts`):** defaults when storage is
empty or broken JSON; a saved value round-trips; picking a preset sets
its values; moving a slider marks the difficulty Custom; an older saved
version gains new keys with their defaults.

Commit: `UI-01 stage 2: pause menu and settings`.
