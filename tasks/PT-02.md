# PT-02: a Foamer that loses its head goes down

Written 2026-11-08 from Paul's playtest: "some enemies keep moving after
they lose their heads. (A blown off head should be death...)"

This changes D12. Today (PLAN.md D12, CONTENT-PLAN 3.2) a Foamer goes
down "when its head and an arm are blown off"; losing the head alone only
slows its turning (`handicaps.noHeadTurnRate`). Paul's new rule: **losing
the head downs it, whatever its health.**

## What to build

- When a Foamer's head part comes off (any cause: darts, explosion, a
  chunk), it goes down at once through the ordinary down path (the same
  topple, fizz and dissolve as a kill, the same events and sounds, the
  same counters), whatever its health. A catastrophic hit that also takes
  the head still pops as it does today.
- Remove the head-and-arm rule (it is now covered). Leave
  `noHeadTurnRate` in the data only if something still reads it;
  otherwise remove it and its uses.
- Bosses: none exist yet. Put a per-type flag `headOffDowns` (default
  true, in the shared defaults of `data/foamers.json`) so a boss can opt
  out later; say so in HANDOFF.
- Tests (new file): for every type with a head, removing the head part
  downs it with health left; a type without a head is unaffected; an
  arm alone does not down it; the kill counts once (no double down when a
  hit takes the head and the last health together).
- Update any existing test that asserted the old rule, and say which in
  HANDOFF.
