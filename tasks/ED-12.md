## What to build, in stages (commit each)

1. **Frame on open.** When a level is opened (stored, built in, imported
   or new), the camera is put behind and above the player start, looking
   the way the start faces, clear of walls. The distance and height are
   numbers in `data/editor.json` `camera`.
2. **Shell to prims.** `shellToPrims(level)` is a pure function: every
   shell box and ramp becomes a solid prim of the same place, size and
   turn, with the colour its material has in `data/materials.json`, and
   the shell is left empty. A ramp's `rise` must give the same slope as
   the prim ramp; if the two shapes cannot match exactly, say so in a
   warning and do not guess. `builtInCopy` runs it after
   `arenaToTriggers`, and its warnings go to the Problems list. A copy
   over the 2,000 prim cap is not converted, with a warning.
3. **No empty saves.** A level is first written to the store on its first
   change, not when it is opened or when New is pressed. Play still saves
   first.

## What changed while building (2026-11-16)

- A shell ramp becomes a **turned box prim**, not a prim ramp. The shell
  ramp is a slab with open space under it; a prim ramp is filled
  underneath, and the levels use that space. The turned box is the same
  slab exactly.
- `src/level/build.ts` was changed, which this card did not list: a piece
  anchored `wall` took its bedrock from shell boxes only, so a copy with
  an empty shell would have dropped it. A solid, unturned box prim now
  counts as a wall too.
- `src/editor/main.ts`: a move no longer snaps the turn (and a turn no
  longer snaps the place). Without this, moving a converted ramp with
  snap on bent it to the nearest 15 degrees.
- The "no empty saves" rule is one flag in `main.ts` and has no unit
  test. It was checked in the browser by counting the store.
