## What is there today

- `data/pickups.json` `health`: the finisher's spray, blobs of `amount`
  10 that hop out, bob, drift to a hurt player and dissolve after
  `lifetime` 30 s, at most `max` 24 live. `Pickups` sprays them
  (`spray...` near line 255 of `src/pickups/pickups.ts`). A blob at full
  health stays on the floor.
- `loadLevelBoxes(level)` builds the level's `darts-*` ammo boxes and (LVL-05)
  the `blaster` pickups. A restart clears the pickups and calls it again,
  so the file's pickups come back on a retry.
- The finisher (`src/game/finisher.ts`) is the only source of health.

## What to build

1. **Placed health.** A level file entry
   `{ "id": "...", "type": "health", "amount": 25, "pos": [x, y, z] }`
   builds a placed health pickup: bigger than a finisher blob, on the floor
   (no hop), turning and bobbing like the ammo box, never expiring, with
   its own look numbers in a new `data/pickups.json` block (say
   `healthPack`: size, colour from the health blob's cream, collectRadius
   1, turnRate, bob, max). Walked over, it heals `amount` (capped at the
   maximum) and is gone; at full health it stays. It comes back on a
   restart like the ammo boxes. The validator accepts the entry and rejects
   one with no positive `amount`.
2. **Melee kills drop health.** A Foamer killed by a melee hit (a combo
   hit or a charged swing; not a dart, not a blast) sprays health blobs the
   way the finisher does, with its own numbers in `data/melee.json`
   (`killDrop`: `count`, `amount`; start at 2 blobs of 5). The finisher's
   own spray is unchanged. If the melee kill path is shared with the
   finisher, make sure one kill does not spray twice.
3. **Act 1 placements.** In 1-2, 1-3 and 1-4, two or three placed health
   pickups each: in the breathers and checkpoints after an arena, never
   inside a locked arena, on the critical path or a short step off it.
   None in the Pit. Each `pos` is a start value Paul will move; list them
   in HANDOFF with what each sits next to.
