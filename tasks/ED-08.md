## What to build, in stages (commit each)

1. **The document.** `LevelDoc` adds, changes, removes, duplicates, undoes
   and redoes `triggers` like the other kinds (same id space). A trigger
   that names an id which is then deleted keeps the name; the Problems
   list already says it is unknown.
2. **The Logic group and inspector.** The outliner gains a Logic group
   listing triggers by id with a one line summary ("enter zone-1: show 2,
   spawn 3 F01"). New trigger from the palette (no position: it is not in
   the world). The inspector edits: the when (a kind list: start, enter,
   killed, pressed, taken, after; then its fields as lists of the ids that
   exist of the right kind: zones for enter, placed Foamers and spawn
   action ids for killed with an optional count, buttons, pickups,
   triggers and seconds), the do list (add, remove, move up and down; each
   row a kind list and its fields: spawn with id, Foamer type, count,
   spawn points ticked, promoted, hold seconds; wake; show; hide; open;
   close; message text and seconds; checkpoint position and yaw, with a
   "use the camera's spot" button), and repeat.
3. **Link.** With a trigger selected, a Link button and the L key: the
   next clicks in the view add the clicked thing to the field that takes
   it (a zone to enter; a Foamer to killed or wake; a prim to show or hide
   of the do row being edited; a door to open or close), Esc ends linking.
   Linked things are outlined in the view while the trigger is selected,
   so Paul can see what it acts on.
4. **The Arena button.** One click on a selected zone builds the usual
   fight as editable triggers, the shape of `ed-07.json`: a lock trigger
   (enter the zone: show the selected blocker prims, spawn wave 1), one
   trigger per further wave (killed all of the wave before), and a clear
   trigger (killed all: hide the blockers, open the doors picked). A small
   dialog asks for the blockers (ticked from the prims marked hidden), the
   spawn points (ticked), the waves (Foamer type and count per wave, add
   and remove) and the doors to open. Messages are left out (the text is
   Paul's to write).
5. **Show the hidden.** Hidden prims draw see-through in the editor so a
   blocker can be seen and clicked; an eye in the toolbar hides all
   hidden things for a look at the level as it starts.
