## What was found (reviewer, 2026-11-08)

- **B07 cannot explode on the range:** `src/range/main.ts` builds
  `new DartImpacts(darts, debris, voxelTargets)` with no
  `ExplosionSystem`. The game pages pass one (`src/game/game.ts` around
  line 240: `new ExplosionSystem(world, debris, [voxelMesh])`, then
  `new DartImpacts(..., explosion)`). So on the range a launcher round,
  B05's explosive mod and B06's every fifth shot all land as plain darts.
- **The menu:** the panel lives in `#overlay`, shown only while the
  pointer is free. The blaster row and the mod row are rebuilt on every
  click. `applyInfiniteAmmoHooks()` overwrites every blaster's `onShot`
  after each mod toggle. Find why the clicks and the mod toggles fail
  (reproduce in a test first where you can), then build the new menu so
  it cannot happen.

## What to build

**Stage 1: blasters and explosions.**
- The player starts with all eight blasters (the blaster `order` in
  `data/blasters.json`). Keys 1 to 8 select, the wheel cycles (down next,
  up previous), as on the arena page.
- Magazines and reloads work as in the game, so a time to kill includes
  reloads. Reserve ammo never runs out on the range (refill the reserve,
  not the magazine). Say in HANDOFF that this is your reading.
- Build an `ExplosionSystem` and pass it to `DartImpacts` exactly as
  `game.ts` does, so B07, B05's explosive mod and B06 explode on the range.
- Test: on the range setup, a B07 round hitting the floor near a Foamer
  calls the explosion (it damages the Foamer or carves the floor), where
  before it did not.

**Stage 2: the line of Foamers.**
- One of every Foamer type in `data/foamers.json` (F01 to F13 today; read
  the list from the data, do not hard-code it), in a straight line across
  the range, facing the player, `lineup.distanceMetres` (start 15) from
  the player's start and `lineup.spacingMetres` (start 4) apart, all in
  `data/range.json`.
- **They stand still:** no brain, no walking, no attacks, no projectiles,
  no healing (F13), no flying off (F10 hovers where it is). They still
  take damage, lose parts, go down, pop and dissolve exactly as in the
  game, because that is what Paul is testing. Find the smallest way to
  do this in the Foamer system (a per-Foamer `held` flag the system
  checks before the brain and movement steps is one way); that one change
  in `src/foamers/foamers.ts` is allowed. Keep it to a few lines. Every
  other test must stay unchanged.
- A small label above each Foamer: its type and name (from the data) and
  its health now. Plain HTML or a sprite, cheap.
- **Respawn** puts the whole line back, fresh. Key `R` is reload, so use
  the menu button and key `T`.

**Stage 3: the readout and the menu.**
- **Time to kill:** for each Foamer, from the first hit on it to the
  moment it goes down (or pops): the blaster, the mods on, the seconds,
  the shots fired at it (trigger pulls that hit it), the distance. Show
  the last five kills in a small corner panel, newest at the top.
- **The menu** (Escape frees the pointer and shows it, as now): the
  active blaster's name and its mod toggles, one button each, lit when
  on; and Respawn. Nothing else on the default page. A click on a mod
  must change the blaster at once and show it lit; a second click turns
  it off. Click the canvas to play again.
- **The old tools:** the benchmark switches, the report, the frame graph,
  the spawn buttons and the type picker are not on the default page. If
  they are used for performance work (`data/range.json` `bench`, the
  report), keep them behind `range.html?bench` rather than delete them.
- Tests: a mod toggle from the menu changes `loadout.modsOf`; a second
  toggles it back; Respawn restores every Foamer to full health; a held
  Foamer does not move or attack over 10 s next to the player; the
  time-to-kill record has the right blaster and a time.
