Read first: `AGENTS.md`, the plan's item 4, `src/render/surfaces.ts`,
`data/surfaces.json`, `src/editor/files.ts` (`skyImageDataUrl`: how an
uploaded image is scaled and kept as a data: URL), `src/level/format.ts`,

`src/editor/terrain-editor.ts`, the surface select in
`src/editor/inspector.ts`.

Build, one commit per step:
1. **Format and validator.** `TextureAsset { id, name, image (data: URL),
   scale }` and `Level.textures?: TextureAsset[]` in `src/level/format.ts`.
   Caps in `data/editor.json` `textures` (maxCount 16, maxSize 512 px a
   side, maxBytes 300000 each). The validator checks them and that a prim's
   `surface`, a physics object's and a terrain layer's may be `tex:<id>` of
   an existing texture. Tests.
2. **Resolution.** In `src/render/surfaces.ts`: `setLevelTextures(list)`
   registers a level's uploads; `surfaceTexture("tex:<id>")` and
   `surfaceMaterial` resolve them (an image texture, RepeatWrapping, sRGB,
   tinted by the colour like the drawn ones), and `SURFACES` lookups for
   scale work for them. The game calls it before building the level
   (`src/level/build.ts`), the editor whenever the level changes.
3. **Editor.** A Textures section in the level inspector (nothing
   selected): Upload (a file input, png or jpg), each texture's name,
   scale (metres a repeat) and Delete. An upload may be a tileset: a
   "tiles across" and "tiles down" pair (default 1 and 1) cuts it into that
   many textures, each its own `TextureAsset` ("name 1", "name 2", ...).
   Images are scaled down to the cap with a canvas, as the sky is. Uploads
   appear in the Paint panel's swatches, the prim surface select and the
   terrain layer selects.
