## What was found (reviewer)

- **F10 jet:** `src/audio/foamer-sounds.ts` about line 109 starts a loop,
  `F10-jet` for the F10 and `F06-hum` for the F06, for as long as the
  Foamer lives. Remove the F10's loop only. Keep the F06 hum.
- **Footsteps:** the player's step is `PL-step-hard` (`data/sounds.json`
  volume 0.7, played from `src/audio/game-sounds.ts` about line 286).
  Paul: about 80% quieter, so volume 0.14.
- **Weapon switch:** `src/audio/game-sounds.ts` about lines 169 to 174
  plays `BX-holster` at the start of a switch and `BX-draw` at the swap.
  Remove both calls.
- **B04 flywheel:** `B04-rev-loop`, started in `game-sounds.ts` about line
  186 while B04 is the active blaster. Remove the loop and whatever only
  served it (its volume follow, its stop calls). Keep `B04-fire`.
- **Foamer dying:** after a Foamer goes down, `foamer-sounds.ts` plays
  `FX-fizz-out` about a second later (about line 95): 3 seconds of
  crackle noise, the "speakers breaking". Remove it and the fizz queue
  that only served it. Keep `FX-topple` (the fall) and `FX-pop`.
- **The Standard (B06):** `"spinUp": 0.25` in its `data/blasters.json`
  entry is the delay before the first shot. Set it to 0 and make sure an
  `auto` blaster with spinUp 0 fires on the first step the trigger is
  held (no division by zero, no wait for wheels). If the `B06-spin`
  sound then plays for nothing, remove its use too.

Leave the sound definitions in `data/sounds.json` (Paul's recordings
will reuse the IDs); remove only the code that plays them, except the
footstep volume, which is a data change. Note each in `design/SOUNDS.md`
("not played since PT-04, Paul").
