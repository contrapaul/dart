## What to build, in stages (commit each)

1. **Voxel breakables.** A prim where `isVoxelBreakable` is true is not
   drawn as a mesh and gets no prim collider: it goes into the level's
   voxel volume like a piece, cut into blocks of at most 3.2 m, a 90 or
   270 degree turn about Y swapping its x and z size (turns about X or Z
   swap the matching axes). Its anchor: "floor" when its bottom touches a
   solid prim or shell top within 0.05 m, else "none". Its colour: the
   voxel palette is ids in `data/materials.json`; if `materials.ts` can
   take extra colours at load without breaking ids (check, and test it),
   register one per distinct breakable colour; otherwise use the nearest
   foam material by colour and say so in HANDOFF. A hidden voxel
   breakable is not supported yet: build it as a hit point breakable
   instead and say so.
2. **Hit point breakables.** `src/level/breakables.ts`: every other
   breakable prim is its own mesh and colliders (as ED-01 builds a hidden
   prim). Darts, blasts and melee hits on it subtract damage (the same
   damage numbers they deal Foamers; find where each is computed). At 0 it
   is gone (mesh hidden, colliders off) and bursts foam chunks through
   `DebrisSystem` (capped already), a count in `data/destruction.json` by
   its volume, capped. A restart brings it back. `setShown` for hidden ones
   keeps working (a trigger may show a breakable blocker).
3. Add three breakables to `data/levels/ed-01.json` (edit by hand): an
   upright foam box wall, a box turned 45 degrees with 60 hit points, and a
   cylinder with 40.
