## What was found (reviewer, 2026-11-08)

- **Nothing pops from darts:** `catastrophic.popTypes` is `[]` (Paul's
  2026-11-01 rule: no starter dart pops). A pop needs 40% of max health
  in 0.1 s from those types only, so in play only explosions can pop.
- **The chunk finish is narrow:** only tiers Heavy, Elite and Boss (or a
  weakened Foamer), only a blaster with a `chunk` block, only inside its
  `rangeMetres`, and only with 25% of max health in the one shot.
- **B03 is weak:** 8 `standard` darts a barrel, damage 1 each, so a whole
  barrel is at most 8. The F05 has 30 health, the F07 45.
- **No dart falls off with distance:** damage is the same at any range.
- **Pierce:** only `precision` (1) and `precisionHeavy` (3) pierce. The
  B01 `slug` has `pierce: 0`, though CONTENT-PLAN section 4 says B01 slugs
  "punch holes in enemies" and pierce through.

## What to build

**Stage 1: the impact scale.** Replace the pop rule and the chunk rule's
gates with one number, worked out once when a shot kills a Foamer:

- `overkill` = the killing shot's damage on this Foamer (all its darts,
  inside `catastrophic.window`) minus the health it had left before that
  shot. Never below 0.
- `near` = 1 when the hit is within the blaster's `impact.fullMetres` of
  the muzzle, falling in a straight line to 0 at `impact.zeroMetres`.
  Defaults for every blaster in the shared data; each blaster may set its
  own. An explosion's kill uses `near` = 1 (its damage already falls off).
- `impact` = (`overkill` / max health) x `near`.

Then, all thresholds in `data/foamers.json` `catastrophic`:

- `impact` below `chunkAt` (start 0.5): it goes down, the ragdoll pushed
  harder as `impact` grows.
- From `chunkAt` to `popAt` (start 1.5): the chunk finish, any tier, the
  hole's radius and the debris count growing with `impact`.
- `popAt` and above: it pops, the burst's push and chunk count growing
  with `impact` up to the existing debris cap (the cap stays hard).

This replaces `popTypes`, `share` and the chunk's `tiers`, `minShare` and
`finishWeakened` gates; remove what nothing reads any more. Paul's
2026-11-01 "no starter dart pops" is replaced by this (his 2026-11-08
words above): say so in section 14.

**Stage 2: B03 and the slug.**
- B03 fires its own pellet type `buck` (a new type in `data/darts.json`,
  like `standard` but a damage line `buck` start 3 in the `damage`
  block), 8 a barrel, and `impact` fullMetres start 3, zeroMetres start
  12. With these, point blank: one barrel on an F01 (health 6) is impact
  3 (a big pop); both barrels on an F05 (30) kill with impact 0.6 (a
  chunk). Tune only if a test shows these are not what happens, and say
  so.
- B01's `slug` gets `pierce` start 1, per CONTENT-PLAN 4. If B02's heavy
  mod uses the same `slug` dart type, give B01's slug its own type rather
  than make B02 pierce, and say so.

**Stage 3: punch-through you can see.**
- Check, in a test, that a B05 precision dart passes through the first
  Foamer of three in a line and hits the second, and that a precisionHeavy
  passes through all three. Fix it if it does not.
- A dart that pierces a Foamer carves its hole right through the body
  (entry and exit), so the punch-through is visible.
- On the range (PT-01), add a short row of three F01s one behind another
  (`lineup.pierceRow` in `data/range.json`), so Paul can try pierce. It
  respawns with the rest.
