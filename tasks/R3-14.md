# R3-14: weapon tuning on the range

Paul, 2026-11-28: "Player weapon details should also be tunable." Plan:
`docs/plan/PLAN-GPU0-tuning.md` items 12 to 14. Owner: Inkling on a V100
(role `r3-14`). Reviewer: Claude.

The lead has built the store: read `src/core/tuning.ts` first (`tuning`,
`tuneBlaster`, `resetTuning`, `exportTuning`, `onTuningChange`) and
`tests/core/tuning.test.ts`, then `resolveSpec` in `src/weapons/blaster.ts`,
`data/blasters.json`, `data/melee.json`, and the range page
(`range.html`, `src/range/main.ts`, `src/range/menu.ts`).

Build, one commit per step:
1. **The fields, as data**, `src/range/blaster-fields.ts`: the numbers a
   blaster has, grouped (Fire: mode-specific fireInterval, spinUp,
   dartsPerShot, spreadDeg, recoil; Ammo: magSize, reserveMax,
   reserveStart, reloadTime, primeTime; Movement: fireMoveShare; Damage:
   the dart type's damage lives in data/darts.json and is not per blaster:
   leave it out and say so), each with path, label, step, min, max, and the
   value in force. Tests.
2. **The panel**, `src/range/tune-panel.ts`: a "Tune" section in the
   range's panel for the held blaster: every field, changed ones marked
   with a reset beside them, edits through `tuneBlaster` (so the editor,
   the arena and play use them too), "Reset this blaster", "Reset all
   tuning" (asks first), "Export tuning" (downloads `exportTuning()` as
   `tuning.json`). The held blaster picks up the change at once: rebuild it
   with `resolveSpec` on `onTuningChange`, keeping its ammo where it can.
3. **Every workspace**: a small "Tuning: changed" note with a Reset button
   on the arena's pause panel when this browser's override is not empty
   (`localTuning()`), so a student on a school computer can put the game
   back.

Files: yours: `src/range/blaster-fields.ts`, `src/range/tune-panel.ts`,
`tests/range/tune-panel.test.ts`, this card. Small hooks: `range.html`,
`src/range/main.ts`, the pause panel (find it under `src/ui/` or
`src/game/`), `src/weapons/loadout.ts` if the held blaster needs a rebuild
hook. Not yours: `src/core/tuning.ts`.
