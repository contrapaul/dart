# R3-10: the Maps screen

Paul, 2026-11-28: "a save/load feature that shows me the maps I've been
working on." Plan: `docs/plan/PLAN-GPU0-tuning.md` item 9. Owner: Inkling on
a V100 (role `r3-10`). Reviewer: Claude.

Read first: `AGENTS.md`, the plan's item 9, `src/editor/store.ts`,
`src/editor/open.ts`, the Open dialog and `scheduleSave` / `saveNow` in
`src/editor/main.ts`, `view.picture` in `src/editor/view.ts` (ED-23 takes an
asset's picture with it).

Build, one commit per step:
1. **A picture with each save**: `StoredLevel` gains `picture?: string` (a
   small JPEG data: URL, 192 by 108, from `view.picture` of the whole level
   from the opening view), written when the level is saved, at most once
   every 10 seconds so typing does not redraw it each key. Older stored
   levels without one still list. Pure parts tested.
2. **The Maps screen**, `src/editor/maps-screen.ts`: a dialog opened by the
   top bar's Open button (it replaces the old Open dialog): a grid of cards,
   newest first, each with the picture, the name, when it was last saved, a
   "Current" mark on the open one, and Open, Duplicate, Export and Delete
   (Delete asks first, and the open level cannot be deleted from here). A
   search box filters by name. Then the built-in levels, opened as copies,
   as the Open dialog does now. A "New map" card at the start.
3. **Saved or not**: the status line says "Saving" and then "Saved" as now;
   the Maps screen shows the open map's state too.

Files: yours: `src/editor/maps-screen.ts`, `src/editor/store.ts`,
