# PT-05: explosions that blow Foamers apart

Written 2026-11-09 from Paul's second playtest:

"The launcher ball blasts straight up, and while large damage is applied,
there is little visible impact on mobs. Damage is applied directly but a
hit next to a weaker mob should result in a huge chunk of that mob blasting
apart, up to total obliteration. Currently they fall over. The only way to
cause visible damage is a direct hit, which only results in minor visible
damage at the impact site. The explosion should be a sphere, blocked by
terrain. Mobs hit by the 'shockwave' that live currently teleport to the
side, which looks terrible. Of note, the standard's explosive mod barely
seems to blast more than a normal round, as do other explosive mods. These
must behave like small launcher explosions: pushing nearby enemies and
causing much larger damage where hit."

## What was found (reviewer)

`explode()` in `src/weapons/explosion.ts`:

1. **Carves only the level.** Step 1 carves `this.targets` (level
   volumes). Foamer part volumes are never carved, so a blast leaves no
   mark on a Foamer.
2. **Damage ignores walls.** Step 2 damages every Foamer whose centre is
   within `rangeMetres`, through anything.
3. **No chunk from a blast.** `blastDamage` calls `resolveKill(f, null)`.
   With no shot, PT-03's chunk band falls through to a plain down, so a
   blast kill is a pop or a fall, never a chunk.
4. **The teleport.** A survivor is shoved by one `knockStep` call that
   moves the whole `pushMetres * fall` (up to 2.5 m) in one physics step.
5. **"Blasts straight up":** step 3 pushes debris with `dy / d + 0.5`,
   and the burst may be upward too. Find out what Paul is seeing (the
   round's own path after it hits, or the chunks) and make the blast
   throw outward from the point, in every direction a sphere would.
6. **The explosive mods** use the `dart` profile: carve 0.6, damage 12,
   range 2.5, `pushMetres` 0. No push at all, and little more than a dart.

## What to build

**Stage 1: the sphere, walls, and the shove.**
- A Foamer is in the blast if any part of it is within `rangeMetres` of
  the point (not only its feet centre) and a ray from the point to that
  part is not blocked by the level (fixed bodies; Foamers and loose
  debris do not block). Use the nearest unblocked part's distance for the
  falloff.
- Replace the one-step shove with a knockback over time: the blast gives
  the Foamer a horizontal velocity (and a little lift if the data says
  so) that its own movement controller spends over the next steps, with
  a decay in data, a wall stopping it as `knockStep` does. No jump in
  position on any one step bigger than its speed allows. Put the numbers
  in `data/explosion.json` per profile (`knockSpeed`, `knockDecay`).
- Debris and broken pieces are pushed outward from the point in 3D
  (the sphere), not mainly up.
- Tests: a wall between the point and a Foamer stops the damage; a
  Foamer whose arm is in range but whose feet are not still takes it;
  a survivor's position changes by no more than `knockSpeed * dt` (plus
  its own walk) on any step and ends up pushed away.

**Stage 2: carving and obliteration.**
- The blast carves every Foamer part inside its sphere, the way it
  carves the level, the hole's size from the profile (`foamerCarveRadius`
  in data), so a blast next to a Foamer always leaves a visible hole.
- A blast kill uses the PT-03 impact rule with the blast's damage on
  that Foamer as the shot and near 1, and then:
  - in the chunk band: a chunk centred on the side facing the blast,
    the hole growing with impact (as PT-03's chunk does for darts);
  - at popAt and above: a pop, the burst thrown outward from the blast
    point, not straight up;
  - far above popAt (`obliterateAt` in data, start 3): total
    obliteration, every part popped into foam chunks and nothing left,
    inside the debris caps.
- Tests: a launcher round 1 m from an F01 obliterates it; 2.5 m from an
  F05 chunks it on the near side; a surviving F07 has fewer voxels on
  the near side after the blast.

**Stage 3: the explosive mods.**
- B05's explosive mod and B06's every fifth shot are small launcher
  blasts: a `dart` profile with a push and enough damage at the point
  that a hit on a Foamer does much more than a plain dart. Start values:
  carve 0.6, damage at the centre 20, range 2.2, a shove. B07's cluster
  bombs the same. Say the numbers in HANDOFF for Paul to tune.
- Test: a B06 fifth shot on an F05 does at least twice the damage of a
  plain mega dart and pushes a neighbouring F01.
