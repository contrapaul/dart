   (the ids inside are renamed on placing, as a paste does). Make four that
   play differently: a square room, a ring round a central block, a long
   hall, an open yard with low cover. Labels describe the shape; Paul may

   checked with the asset reader; a test that every template places into a
   new level and the result passes `validateLevel` with no errors.
3. **The panel**: the Assets dialog gets a "Templates" section above the
   saved ones, each with its picture name and a Place button that arms it
   (`armAsset`), so it places under the cursor like any asset.
