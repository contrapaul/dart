## What to build

1. **Rules** (`hazard-rules.ts`, no Three.js): damage is dealt in ticks of
   `defaults.tickSeconds` (add it, start 0.25) so hits read on the HUD; a
   body inside one or more hazards takes the highest `dps` of them, times
   the tick; leaving and coming back does not reset to a free tick (keep an
   accumulator per body that only drains while inside). The slow is the
   lowest `slow` of the hazards it is in (1 when none). `hurtsFoamers`
   false skips Foamers.
2. **System** (`hazards.ts`): `new Hazards(prims, parent: THREE.Object3D)`
   takes the level's hazard prims, builds each mesh from `primGeometry` and
   `primMatrix` with a see-through material from its look (colour, opacity,
   emissive glow; `depthWrite` false; double sided so it reads from inside;
   `transparent` true) and caches each inverse matrix. `setShown(id,
   shown)` for hidden ones (a hidden hazard does nothing). `update(dt,
   player: THREE.Vector3, foamers: { id: string; pos: THREE.Vector3 }[])`
   returns `{ playerDamage: number; slow: number; foamerDamage: Map<string,
   number> }` using `primContains` on the player's feet raised 0.3 m (a
   body standing in a shallow lake is in it). Nothing is allocated per
   frame (reuse the vectors and the map). `dispose()`.
3. A gentle surface shimmer is optional and cheap only: a time uniform on
   the emissive, no extra passes.
