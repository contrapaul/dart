## What to build, in stages (commit each)

1. **The frame.** Play saves, then adds an `<iframe>` that covers the
   view and the panels, with the URL `playUrl` already builds plus
   `&frame=1`. The editor page does not navigate. Closing removes the
   frame from the page (do not hide and keep it), and gives the keyboard
   back to the editor.
2. **No title card.** With `frame=1` the arena page shows only "Click to
   play" and starts on the first click in the frame. No Make a level,
   Play a level or Settings buttons.
3. **Esc comes back.** With `frame=1`, when mouse look is released (Esc)
   the arena page posts a message to its parent and the editor closes the
   frame. No pause panel. The editor accepts the message only from its
   own origin and its own frame. Finishing the level or being knocked out
   keeps the game's own screens, each with a Back to the editor button
   that posts the same message.
4. **Keys and the button.** Tab plays from the player start, Shift+Tab
   from the camera. P and Shift+P still work. The Play button moves to
   the middle of the top bar and is drawn as a play triangle with the
   word Play. Help text updated.
5. **Measure.** Record in the handoff entry the time from pressing Tab to
   the "Click to play" frame being drawn, for a new level and for 1-2
   opened as a copy, on the dev server and on `npm run preview`.

Opened without `frame=1`, `arena.html` behaves exactly as today, so My
levels and the old links keep working.
