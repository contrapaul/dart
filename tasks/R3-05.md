## Result

Done. Three steps, one commit each, all green.

What works:
- `tools/render-surfaces.ts` writes `design/surfaces/sheet.png`: every surface
  drawn 2 by 2 (so the tile seam shows) in three palette tints (foam orange,
  blue and lime from data/materials.json), with its name on the left. The
  sheet draws the built-ins and the generated surfaces; the generated names
  have no palette entry yet (see below), so they are labelled by painter name.
  Proof: `npx tsx tools/render-surfaces.ts` rewrites the sheet with all 17 rows
  and no errors.
- Eight new surfaces in `src/render/painters-more.ts` (`MORE_PAINTERS`), all
  tile at 256 px (the 2 by 2 sheet and the per-surface 2 x 2 renders show no
  seam), all greys, seeded PRNG only:
  cobbles, dirt, wood-floor, foam-tiles, grate, carpet, gravel, padding. The
  per-pixel speckle is hash-based (periodic with the image), so no edge speckle
  draws a seam.
- `tests/render/painters-more.test.ts`: every generated painter draws, greys,
  alpha 255, deterministic, not flat, at 256 px and at the sheet's own 128 px.
  Proof: `npx vitest run tests/render/painters-more.test.ts` (5 tests pass) and
  `.swarm/preflight.sh` green.

Which of the nine existing surfaces do not match (from the sheet and the 2 by 2
renders, not just a fresh-eye pass):
- planks: the grain `sin((x + row * 37) / 9)` has period ~56.6 px, which does
  not divide 256, so the wavy band steps at the vertical boundary of each tile.
- concrete: blotches are drawn once, no wrap, so they are cut at the tile edges
  and the seam shows at both joins; contrast is flat (185 base, 0.82 blotches).
- grass: blades are drawn once, no wrap, so they are cut at the tile edges; the
  2 by 2 shows faint streak discontinuities; low contrast, reads as noise.
- sand: dune streaks use period 40 to 100 px, which does not divide 256, so
  streaks visibly jump at the boundary (most noticeable); otherwise fine.
- rock: blobs are drawn once, no wrap, cut at both joins; reads as a dark blob
  pattern at 3 m scale.
- brick: matches (staggered rows continue, no visible seam).
- tiles: matches.
- metal: matches.
- stripes: matches (perfectly tileable diagonal).

Fixes for the nine: none inside my files (the nine live in
