## Build, one commit per step

1. **The mover (game, pure part first).** `src/level/movers.ts`: a
   `Movers` class with `start(ids, by, turnDeg, seconds)` and
   `update(dt)`. Each moving thing has a start transform and a target
   (position plus `by`, yaw plus `turn` about its own centre), eased with
   smoothstep over `seconds`. A move on a thing that is already moving
   starts from where it is now. No allocation in `update`. Unit test the
   maths headless (`tests/level/movers.test.ts`).
2. **Moving the real things.** In the arena page's TriggerHost implement
   `move`: prims (mesh and colliders; a moved prim's colliders move with it
   as kinematic position based bodies, set each step with
   `setNextKinematicTranslation` and `setNextKinematicRotation`), props,
   buttons, and any later kind through a small registry
   (`registerMovable(id, apply)`), so ED-32 lights, ED-33 physics objects
   and ED-35 models can plug in without editing your file. A merged prim
   mesh (prims are merged per colour in `src/level/prims.ts`) cannot move
   one prim: a prim named by any `move` in the level's triggers is built as
   its own mesh instead. Find those ids once at load.
3. **Editor: the move row.** In `inspector-trigger.ts` a `move` row:
   the ids (Link, like show and hide), `by` as three numbers, `turn`
   degrees, `seconds`. The trigger summary already says "move ...".
   The do kind picker lists `move`.
4. **Editor: buttons.** `buttons` is an entry kind now. A Button tool in the
   left tool bar (Logic group). Clicking a surface places a button on it:
   `pos` at the hit point, `facing` the hit face's normal (any unit vector:
   relax the validator's axis-only rule in `src/level/validate.ts` and make
   `buildButton` turn the plate to any normal), `on` the id of the thing
   clicked (a prim, model or physics object), nothing when it is the
   floor of the level. The marker is the real button look from
   `data/buttons.json`. Inspector: position, facing (read only), `on`
   (pick from a list or clear), `use` (tick: needs the use key). The
   `pressed` when in the trigger inspector lists the buttons.
5. **Game: editor buttons.** Buttons in an editor level: no `action` is
   fine; a press fires `pressed` (already wired); with `use`, a press needs
   the player within reach looking at it and the use key (find the use key
   in `data/input.json`, the stations use it). A button with `on` is moved
   by a move on that thing, and hidden and shown with it.
