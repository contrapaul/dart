## Build, one commit per step

1. **The mover (game, pure part first).** `src/level/movers.ts`: a
   `Movers` class with `start(ids, by, turnDeg, seconds)` and
   `update(dt)`. Each moving thing has a start transform and a target
   (position plus `by`, yaw plus `turn` about its own centre), eased with
   smoothstep over `seconds`. A move on a thing that is already moving
   starts from where it is now. No allocation in `update`. Unit test the
   maths headless (`tests/level/movers.test.ts`).
2. **Moving the real things.** In the arena page's TriggerHost implement
   `move`: prims (mesh and colliders; a moved prim's colliders move with it
   as kinematic position based bodies, set each step with
   `setNextKinematicTranslation` and `setNextKinematicRotation`), props,
   buttons, and any later kind through a small registry
   (`registerMovable(id, apply)`), so ED-32 lights, ED-33 physics objects
   and ED-35 models can plug in without editing your file. A merged prim
   mesh (prims are merged per colour in `src/level/prims.ts`) cannot move
   one prim: a prim named by any `move` in the level's triggers is built as
   its own mesh instead. Find those ids once at load.
3. **Editor: the move row.** In `inspector-trigger.ts` a `move` row:
   the ids (Link, like show and hide), `by` as three numbers, `turn`
   degrees, `seconds`. The trigger summary already says "move ...".
   The do kind picker lists `move`.
4. **Editor: buttons.** `buttons` is an entry kind now. A Button tool in the
   left tool bar (Logic group). Clicking a surface places a button on it:
   `pos` at the hit point, `facing` the hit face's normal (any unit vector:
   relax the validator's axis-only rule in `src/level/validate.ts` and make
   `buildButton` turn the plate to any normal), `on` the id of the thing
   clicked (a prim, model or physics object), nothing when it is the
   floor of the level. The marker is the real button look from
   `data/buttons.json`. Inspector: position, facing (read only), `on`
   (pick from a list or clear), `use` (tick: needs the use key). The
   `pressed` when in the trigger inspector lists the buttons.
5. **Game: editor buttons.** Buttons in an editor level: no `action` is
   fine; a press fires `pressed` (already wired); with `use`, a press needs
   the player within reach looking at it and the use key (find the use key
   in `data/input.json`, the stations use it). A button with `on` is moved
   by a move on that thing, and hidden and shown with it.

## Result

### What works

- `src/level/movers.ts`: `Movers.start/update/reset`, smoothstep easing, a
  move on a moving thing starting from where it is, `turnAbout` for a point
  round a centre, and riders so a mounted thing follows its owner.
  `Movers.register(id, apply)` and `Movers.ride(ownerId, apply)` are the
  registry; `registerMovables(movers, list)` is the arena page's helper.
- `src/arena/extras.ts`: `move` in the TriggerHost runs `Movers.start` and
  the step hook runs `movers.update(dt)`. Prims, props and buttons register.
  A button with `on` rides on its thing and is hidden, shown and reset with
  it. A press fires the trigger whose when is that button (first press
  only). `movers` is on `ArenaExtras`, so ED-32, ED-33 and ED-35 plug in
  with one `register` call and nothing to edit here.
- `src/level/prims.ts`: a prim named by a move is built as its own mesh,
  with its colliders on a kinematic position based body driven by
  `setNextKinematicTranslation` and `setNextKinematicRotation`. A prop id
  covers its `<id>#n` parts, which turn about the prop's foot as one.
- `src/level/buttons.ts`: buttons carry their plate, current normal,
  `move` and `setShown`. A `use` button is skipped by a dart, a swing and a
  step, and is pressed by `pressWithUse` (in reach, in view, once a press).
- `src/level/validate.ts`: an editor button needs no action, and its facing
  may be any unit vector.
- Editor: the move row (ids with Link, by, turn, seconds) and move in the
  do picker; `src/editor/buttons-editor.ts` with the Button tool in Logic,
  the real plate and cap marker from `data/buttons.json`, and the inspector
  (position, facing read only, `on`, `use`). The `pressed` when lists the
  buttons. Button icon, help row and outliner group.

### Look at in a browser (there was no browser here)

- `editor.html`: Logic, the Button tool. Click a floor, a wall, a slope and
  the side of a prim. The marker sits flat on the face clicked, the
  inspector shows that facing and the thing it is mounted on, and the use
  tick stays set.
- Play a level whose trigger is "a button is pressed" -> move a prim by
  (0, 2, 0) over 2 seconds. The prim rises eased, and a button mounted on
  it travels and turns with it. Hide that prim: the button goes too.
- A button with `use` takes E while looked at, and a dart, a swing or a
  step leaves it alone. A held E is one press.

### Not done

- Nothing was opened in a browser, so the editor's placement on a slope,
  the use key's feel and the marker's look are unverified there.
- A button that is both named by a move and mounted on a moving thing gets
  two applies; the last one wins. Not guarded.

### Requests

`docs/requests/ed31.md`: breakable and hazard prims need to register with

### Decisions the plan did not settle

- Registry shape: `register(id, apply)` plus `ride(ownerId, apply)`, with
  `registerMovables` as the page's helper, so the rider wiring is in tested
  code and a later kind needs one call.
- A press fires `pressed` on the first press only; a dead click does not
  refire a repeating trigger.
- A `use` button is not pressed by a dart, a swing or a step. Only the use
  key presses it, and it takes the key before a station does.
- A button may also mount on a prop, not only a prim, model or physics
  object, because a prop is built as prims.
- A prop turns about its foot position (its own centre on the horizontal
  axes), so it stays where it was placed.
