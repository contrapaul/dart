## Result

All three steps built.

1. Size is a multiplier (0.5 to 3, step 0.1, tooltip, reset and changed mark in multiplier terms). It still writes the rig's voxelSize (committed voxelSize x multiplier). Tests in tests/editor/tuning-fields.test.ts.
2. Labels are plain words with units ("movement speed (m/s)", "attack range (m)", "wind-up before a hit (s)", "damage (times)"); every field has a title tooltip with one sentence. Every path is kept.
