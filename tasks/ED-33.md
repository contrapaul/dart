## Build, one commit per step

1. **Numbers: `data/physics.json`.** `damageSpeed` (closing speed in m/s
   below which nothing is hurt, start 4), `bounce` default (0.2),
   `friction` (0.7), `dartImpulse` (N s a standard dart gives, start 1.5,
   scaled by the dart type's damage), `blastImpulse` (start 40 at the
   centre, falling with distance), `maxLive` (the cap of awake bodies,
   start 60), `sleepSeconds`. Nothing else as a literal in code.
2. **Game: `src/level/physics-objects.ts`.** One Rapier dynamic body per
   object: box, cylinder or ball collider of its size, mass from `mass`
   (set density so the body's mass matches), restitution `bounce`,
   friction. Drawn with `surfaceMaterial(surface, colour)` from
   `src/render/surfaces.ts`. `asleep` starts it sleeping. Each step copy
   the body's transform to the mesh. Darts push it (an impulse at the hit
   point along the dart's travel); a blast pushes it away from the centre.
   Hitting the player or a Foamer at a closing speed above `damageSpeed`
   deals `damage * (speed - damageSpeed)`, once per contact (use Rapier
   contact events or a per pair cooldown). Breakable ones take dart, blast
   and melee damage the way ED-06 breakables do and shatter into foam
   chunks (reuse the Breakables debris path). Hidden ones are off until
   shown. Restart puts every object back where the level put it. Register
   each with the ED-31 move registry if it exists by then, else note it.
3. **Editor.** A Physics tool in the left tool bar (Things group, an inline
   SVG in `icons.ts`) with a picker for box, cylinder or ball; place on the
   surface like a prim (ghost first). Marker: the shape in its colour.
   Inspector: shape, size, colour, surface, mass (kg), damage, bounce,
   breakable and hit points, asleep, hidden. A "Drop test" note in Help
   says play to see it fall (the editor does not simulate).

### What works, and the command that proves it

`npx vitest run tests/level/physics-objects.test.ts tests/editor/ed33.test.ts`
(11 + 6 tests, all passing). `.swarm/preflight.sh` is green (201 files, 1582
tests, 2 skipped, at the time of writing), and `npm run build` bundles.

The gameplay tests cover: an object falls and rests on a floor; its body
mass equals `mass` for a box, a cylinder and a ball; a dart's impulse shoves
it along the dart's travel; a blast throws it farther at the centre than at
the edge; a fast contact deals `damage * (closing speed - damageSpeed)` once
and a slow one deals nothing; a breakable one shatters at zero hit points
and bursts chunks; restart puts it back whole and asleep; the awake cap
holds with more than `maxLive` bodies. The editor tests cover: the tool's
kind and id prefix, its three shapes, placement standing on the surface, a
validated level file, the marker being the shape in its colour and the
object's size, and the Help row naming the drop test.

### What does not work or is unverified

- No browser was available. A person should look at: placing a box, cylinder
  and ball with the Physics tool (ghost follows the cursor, the marker is the
  shape in colour, the scale handles resize it); Play and watch one fall and
  rest; shoot one with a dart and see it move; set a high mass and a high
  damage and let it fall on the player; tick breakable, empty its hit points
  with darts and see the foam burst; press Play again and see it back where
  the level put it; tick hidden and show it with a trigger.
- The Foamer side of the contact damage is wired (`foamers.parryDamage`) but
  is not covered by a headless test: a Foamer in a test needs the whole
  Foamer system.
- The ED-31 move registry does not exist yet. `PhysicsObjects.moveBy` and
  `turnBy` are there for it; see `docs/requests/ed33.md`.

### Decisions the plan did not settle

- A physics object's `pos` is its centre (as the editor's placeholder marker
  already assumed), not its foot; the Physics tool places it half a size
  along the surface normal so it stands on the floor.
- The awake cap sleeps the bodies past the cap in file order. The cap is read
  every step, so a body that wakes later waits its turn.
- The dart impulse is scaled by `dartDamageOf(type) / dartDamageOf("standard")`,
  both read from `data/foamers.json`, so no new reference number is written.
- Two numbers were added to `data/physics.json` beyond the card's list:
  `sleepSpeed` (the speed at or below which a body counts as resting) and a
  `defaults` block for the editor (size, mass, damage, hit points, shape).
  A "once per contact" rule is separation based, not a timer, so it needs no
  cooldown number.
- A broken breakable is disabled, not rebuilt; restart enables it and puts
  it home. Hidden objects disable the whole body, so they cost nothing while
  off.
- `physics-objects.ts` does not import the player or the Foamers: the page
  hands it a `PhysicsTarget` list each step (position, radius, a `hit`
  callback), which is what keeps it testable headless.
