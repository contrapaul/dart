## Result

Done. The pure bucket, the Bucket mode in the Paint panel and the Alt+click
look-pick are built and committed (e6fb31b..HEAD).

What works:
- `connectedSameLook(level, startId)` (src/editor/bucket.ts) returns the start
  prim and every prim reachable through touching or overlapping boxes (axis-aligned
  bounds of the rotated box, grown by 0.01 m) that wear the same surface and colour.
  Capped at `paint.bucketMax` (new, 500, added to data/editor.json).
- `fillTerrain(t, layerIndex)` puts every vertex of a terrain onto one layer.
- Bucket mode (two buttons at the top of the Paint panel, `PaintSettings.mode`):
  a click on a prim paints every prim `connectedSameLook` finds (one undo step,
  status line "Filled N prims."); a click on the terrain fills the whole terrain
  with the chosen look via `layerForPaint` (one undo step, "Filled the terrain.").
- Alt+click with the Paint tool armed copies the clicked prim's or physics
  object's surface and colour (or, on the terrain, the heaviest layer under the
  cursor) into the settings; the panel shows it and the status line names it.
  Only a click with no drag (the camera's Alt+drag orbit is untouched).
- Tests in tests/editor/bucket.test.ts cover the pure pieces (connectivity,
  plain surface default, rotated bounds, the 500 cap, fillTerrain,
  heaviestLayerAt, lookOf).

Proof: `.swarm/preflight.sh` is green (type check, 218 test files, dash check).
