Read first: `AGENTS.md`, `src/editor/paint-tool.ts` (the panel and
`PaintSettings`), the paint press in `src/editor/main.ts` (search `kind:

(`layerForPaint`, `ensurePaint`).

Build, one commit per step:
1. **Pure flood fill**, `src/editor/bucket.ts`: `connectedSameLook(level,
   startId)`: the start prim and every prim reachable from it through prims
   whose boxes touch or overlap (axis aligned bounds grown by 0.01 m; use
   the rotated bounds) and that wear the same surface and colour as the
   start. A cap from `data/editor.json` `paint.bucketMax` (add it: 500).
   `fillTerrain(t, layerIndex)`: every vertex all that layer. Tests first.
2. **Bucket mode** in the Paint panel: two buttons at the top, Brush and
   Bucket (`PaintSettings.mode`). In Bucket mode a click on a prim paints
   every prim `connectedSameLook` finds, a click on the terrain fills the
   whole terrain with the chosen look (via `layerForPaint`), each one undo
   step, with a status line ("Filled 14 prims.").
3. **Pick up a look**: Alt+click with the Paint tool armed copies the
   clicked prim's or physics object's surface and colour (or, on the
   terrain, the heaviest layer under the cursor) into the settings, and the
   panel shows it. Not with the camera's Alt+drag orbit: only a click with
   no drag.
