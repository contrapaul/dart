## The maths (use exactly this)

The pivot P is the centre of the selection's bounding box at the start of
the drag. The handle's rotation since the drag began is the quaternion R,
its uniform scale s (the scale handle's three values averaged; ignore
non-uniform scale for a selection of two or more).

For every selected thing with position p and its own rotation quaternion Q
(prims and anything with `rot`: from Euler XYZ degrees; things that turn
only by yaw: Q from that yaw about Y):

- new position: P + s * R.apply(p - P)
- new rotation: R * Q. For a prim, back to Euler XYZ degrees
  (`new THREE.Euler().setFromQuaternion(q, "XYZ")`), rounded to 1e-4.
  For a yaw only thing (Foamer `yaw` radians, door, prop `rotY` degrees,
  the start), take the yaw of R * Q: `atan2(2(wy + xz), 1 - 2(y^2 + z^2))`
  on the normalised quaternion, and leave tilt out (they stand upright).
- new size: s * size, for things that have a size (prims, zones, doors,
  physics objects); a model's `scale` times s. Things without a size keep
  theirs.
- A selection that holds any yaw only thing turns about Y only: lock the
  rotate handle to Y while it is selected.

## Build, one commit per step

1. **Pure module** `src/editor/group-transform.ts`:
   `transformSelection(entries, pivot, R, s)` returning the patches, with
   the rules above. Tests first (`tests/editor/ed37.test.ts`): two boxes
   side by side turned 90 degrees about Y swap places round the pivot and
   both gain 90 on Y; a box turned 30 on X then the group turned 45 on Y
   gives the composed Euler; scale 2 doubles sizes and distances from the
   pivot; a Foamer in the group keeps standing upright.
2. **Wire it.** When two or more things are selected (a group, a box
   select, a just placed asset), the handle drag uses it: live while
   dragging (move the drawn objects, as `follow` does now) and one undo
   step at the end. One selected thing behaves as now.
3. **Assets place as one.** An armed asset (ED-23) turns with `[` and `]`
   as one object about its middle before it is placed (the ghost turns),
   using the same function.

## Result

Done, in four commits on `swarm/ed37`.

### What works

- `src/editor/group-transform.ts`: `transformSelection(things, pivot, R, s, move)`
  applies the card's maths: the new place is `P + move + s * R(p - P)`, a thing
  with `rot` gets the composed Euler XYZ in degrees (1e-4), a Foamer and the
  start get the composed yaw in radians, a door and a prop in degrees, and a
  size or a model's `scale` is multiplied by `s`. Untouched things get no
  patch.
- `tests/editor/ed37.test.ts` (9 tests): two boxes swap ends round the pivot
  and both gain 90 on Y, a box at 30 on X then a group at 45 on Y gives
  `[39.2315, 37.7612, -26.5651]` (worked out by hand, not by adding angles),
  scale 2 doubles sizes and distances, a Foamer keeps standing (yaw only, no
  tilt), a prim and a Foamer in one group behave as one, a door takes the turn
  in `rotY` degrees, and an unaltered selection writes nothing.
- Two or more selected things now drag as one: the view's handles sit on the
  middle of the selection's box, the live preview uses the same pivot (and
  keeps upright markers upright), and the document is written once at the end.
  A group that spans kinds (an asset, a box select over prims and markers)
  is one undo step, through `doc.setFields` with whole arrays, so no change to
  `doc.ts` was needed. A selection holding a Foamer, a door, a prop or the
  start gets only the Y ring in rotate mode.
- `[` and `]` on two or more selected things turn them as one about the same
  pivot; a single thing behaves as before.
- An armed asset (ED-23) turns with `[` and `]` as one object about the middle
  of its ghost, the ghost turns as it is moved, and the placed things land
  turned about that same middle in one undo step.
- Help text says both of these.

Proof: `npx vitest run tests/editor/ed37.test.ts --reporter=dot` (9 passed)
and `.swarm/preflight.sh` green (tsc, 1570 passed, dash check) before each
commit.

### To look at in a browser (no browser here)

- Select two or three prims, W/E/R: the handles should sit on the middle of
  the box, a turn should swing the group about that middle, and a scale should
  grow everything and its distance from the middle together.
- Box select over prims and a Foamer, then E: only the Y ring should show, the
  prims should turn and the Foamer should stay upright and swing round.
- Save two or more prims as an asset, arm it, turn it with `[`/`]` over the
  floor: the ghost should turn about its own middle, and the placed asset
  should match the ghost.
- Ctrl+Z after each of those should take the whole drag back in one step,
  including a group that spans kinds.

### Not done, or unverified

- Nothing was run in a browser: the handle wiring, the ghost's turn and the
  live preview are reasoned about and type checked, not watched.
- Steps and corner cases of the Three.js transform controls (a rotate of a
  group that also contains the player start) are not covered by tests.
- Non-uniform scale of a group is deliberately ignored (the plan says one
  number); the last thing's scale axes are averaged.

### Decisions the card did not settle

- The proxy (and so the rotate and scale handles) sits on the middle of a
  multi selection's box rather than on the last thing, so the handles and the
  maths share the same pivot. That is the one visible change to the handles.
- A group move still snaps each thing's place to the grid when snap is on; a
  group turn or scale leaves the places unsnapped so the shape survives.
- The size of every sized thing is multiplied by the average of the three
  handle scale ratios; a size never goes below 1e-4.
- A group drag is written as level fields (whole arrays) through the existing
  `setFields`, which keeps it one undo step across kinds without editing
  `doc.ts`.
- The exit is treated as a thing that does not turn (it has no rotation), so a
  group turn moves it and never adds a `rot` field to it.
