   panel's height (the brush sliders show only in Brush), and since the
   panel is anchored at its bottom, the Brush and Bucket buttons jump under
   the pointer. Keep the panel's height the same in both modes (show the
   brush section in both, disabled with a note in Bucket mode, or move the
   mode buttons to the bottom row), so nothing moves under the pointer.
2. **The brush ring on hover.** With the Paint tool armed in Brush mode,
   hovering the terrain shows the paint brush's ring (its own size, not
   the Terrain tool's), as the Terrain tool shows its own. Reuse the ring
   drawing; do not copy it (export a small helper from terrain-editor.ts
   if needed, that file is yours for this one helper only).
3. **A Bucket hint**: in Bucket mode the tool hint says what a click does
   ("Bucket: click a prim to fill every touching prim of the same look,
   or the terrain to fill it all. Alt+click picks up a look.").

Files: yours: `src/editor/paint-tool.ts`, `tests/editor/paint-panel.test.ts`,
this card. Small hooks: `src/editor/main.ts`, `src/editor/terrain-editor.ts`

## Result

Done. Three commits (888653d, 6caa21b).

What works:
- Panel stays put: the Brush and Bucket buttons moved to the bottom row of the panel, the brush section is always rendered (disabled in Bucket mode, no extra line), so nothing moves under the pointer. Sliders are disabled in Bucket mode.
- Brush ring on hover: with the Paint tool armed in Brush mode, hovering the terrain shows the paint brush's own ring (its own radius and strength), using the exported `layoutRing` helper from terrain-editor.ts (shared with the Terrain tool's ring, not copied). Hidden when the tool is put down or the pointer leaves the view.
