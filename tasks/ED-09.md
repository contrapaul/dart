## What to build, in stages (commit each)

1. **Play from here and back.** P saves and opens
   `arena.html?level=local:<key>&from=editor`; Shift+P adds
   `&start=x,y,z,yaw` from the editor camera (dropped to the floor under
   it). On the arena page, `start` overrides the level's player start for
   that run only, and `from=editor` makes Esc's pause panel show a
   "Back to the editor" button that goes to `editor.html?open=<key>`.
2. **`?open=<key>`.** The editor opens that stored level on load (My
   levels' Edit uses it). Without it, the editor opens the most recently
   saved level, or a new one if there is none.
3. **Open a built-in level.** The Open list's built-in levels are opened
   as a copy through `arenaToTriggers`, so their fights become editable
   triggers; its warnings are shown once in the Problems list.
4. **Help.** A Help button in the top bar opens a panel: the keys, how
   to make a blocker, how the Arena button works, keys and doors, hazards
   and breakables, saving and sharing a file. Plain short sentences, no
   em dashes, no invented story or names.
