Build, one commit per step:
1. **The options**, `LevelLighting` grows optional `gi?: { toneMapping:
   "none" | "aces" | "agx" | "neutral"; exposure: number; skyLight: number;
   bounce: string }` (validator too). Defaults in `data/render.json`
   `look`. A pure `src/render/look.ts` with `toneMappingFor(name)` and the
   defaults merge. Tests.
2. **Light from the sky**: `skyLight` above 0 sets `scene.environment` to a
   PMREM of the level's sky (gradient or image) at that strength
   (`scene.environmentIntensity`), so every surface picks up the sky's
   colour from above and the floor's from below: the cheap global light.
   `bounce` is the hemisphere light's ground colour (the light thrown up
   from the floor). Tone mapping and exposure on the renderer. Same in the
   game and in the editor view, so what Paul sees is what plays. The low
   quality preset turns skyLight off.
3. **Editor**: in the Lighting section: tone mapping (select), exposure,
   sky light and bounce colour, with a "Default" button.

Files: yours: `src/render/look.ts`, `tests/render/look.test.ts`, this
card. Small hooks: `src/render/scene.ts`, `src/editor/view.ts`,
`src/level/lights.ts` (`applyLighting`), `src/level/format.ts`,
