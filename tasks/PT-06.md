## What was found (reviewer)

- `src/weapons/dart-model.ts` builds each type's geometry in code: a
  body plus a tip at `TIP_FRACTION` 0.22 with a `TIP_RATIO` 1.25 bulge,
  10 segments, vertex colours. Paul sees black triangles streaked with
  blue that come to a point: find out why (wrong winding or normals, a
  cone instead of a rounded tip, vertex colours missing on some faces,
  the material). Fix the cause, do not hide it.
- Orientation: the quaternion is set when a dart is fired
  (`darts.ts` about line 286) and when it comes to rest (about 619 and
  694), never while it flies. So a dart keeps its launch angle through
  its arc, and after a bounce it flies backwards or sideways.

## What to build

- **The shape:** a plain cylinder, flat at the back, and a rounded foam
  tip about 15% of the length (`tipShare` 0.15 in data), the tip's
  radius equal to the body's (no bulge), smooth shaded, lit, no black
  faces from any side. Keep each type's `length` and `radius`.
- **The colours (Paul):**
  - `standard`: blue body `#2ec4ff`, reddish orange tip `#ff5a1a`.
  - `mega`: red body `#e8332a`, yellow tip `#ffd23f`.
  - `precision`: yellow body `#ffd23f`, blue tip `#2ec4ff`.
  - `launcher`: a yellow ball `#ffd23f`, the size it has now, using the
    `shape: "ball"` path B08's ball already has.
  - The rest Paul did not name: `precisionHeavy` like precision,
    `buck` like standard, `b01slug` and `slug` keep their colours as
    cylinders, `suction` keeps its colours, `ball` stays as it is. Say so
    in HANDOFF for Paul.
- **Orientation:** every flying dart (not a ball) turns along its
  velocity every step, so its tip leads through the arc and after each
  bounce. A ball does not need to turn. Resting and stuck darts keep the
  rules they have.
- Tests: the geometry of each dart type has its tip at +Z, a flat back,
  every face's normal pointing outward and vertex colours on every
  vertex (body and tip in the data's colours); a dart fired at 45
  degrees up points downward near the end of its arc; after a bounce off
  a wall its +Z axis is within a few degrees of its new velocity.
