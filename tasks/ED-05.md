## What to build, in stages (commit each)

1. **The document.** `LevelDoc` gains the same add, change, remove,
   duplicate, undo and redo for the other kinds: `foamers`, `pickups`,
   `doors`, `spawnPoints`, `zones`, and changes to `playerStart` and
   `exit`. One undo list for all of it. Ids are unique across every kind
   (the validator's single id space): `foamer-1`, `key-2`, `door-3`, ...
   Tested like the prims.
2. **Markers in the view.** Each kind draws as a simple code-built marker
   you can click and move with the same handles (no models need loading):
   a Foamer is a capsule the size of its rig with a nose showing `yaw`,
   coloured by type; a pickup a small box in its kind's colour (keys in
   their red, blue or yellow), a blaster or hand weapon pickup labelled;
   a spawn point a flat ring; a zone and the exit a see-through box (the
   exit green); the player start an arrow showing `yaw`; a door a box in
   its colour (or its key's colour). Rotate on a Foamer, the start or a
   door turns `yaw` or `rotY` only; scale on a zone, a door or the exit
   changes its `size`; the others do not scale.
3. **The palette and the inspector.** The palette gains Foamer, Pickup,
   Door, Spawn point, Zone (and Start and Exit move the one there is).
   The inspector edits each kind's fields: a Foamer's type (a list of the
   rig ids with their names), yaw, promoted, wake (sight or trigger),
   hidden; a pickup's type (ammo by dart type and count, health and its
   amount, blaster and which one, hand weapon and which, key and its
   colour); a door's lock (none, trigger, red, blue, yellow) and colour;
   the start's yaw. The outliner lists every kind in its own group.
4. **Level settings** (shown when nothing is selected, beside the sky):
   the start loadout, blasters to tick (the first ticked is held), hand
   weapons, health.
5. **Problems.** A Problems count in the top bar runs `validateLevel` on
   every change; clicking it lists the errors and warnings, and clicking
   one that names an id selects that thing.
