## What was found (reviewer)

- `data/foamers.json` `test.partDamage.kinds.weapon` is
  `{ hpShare 0.25, hit 1, total 0.5 }`: half of every hit on a club, a
  mace or a sword comes off the Foamer's health.
- F11's `armour.shield` and `armour.shieldBoss` are `damage 0, carve 0`
  inside a 120 degree front arc: a dart there does nothing at all, so
  only two melee parries can break the shield (`meleeParry.parriesToBreak`).

## What to build

- **Weapons:** `kinds.weapon.total` 0. A hit on a weapon part still wears
  that part (its own hp, carve, break-off as today), but never the
  Foamer's health. Check every Foamer with a weapon part (F01 club, F07
  sword, F11 mace and handle, any other) still loses the weapon and its
  attacks when the part breaks.
- **F11's shield:** a dart inside the front arc still does no damage to
  the Seawall's health, but wears the shield part: it takes
  `armour.shield.wear` (start 1, a share of the dart's damage) off the
  shield's own hp and carves a small mark (`armour.shield.carve` start
  0.3). When the shield's hp runs out it breaks off as a foam piece,
  exactly as the parry break does today, and the arc armour goes with
  it. Its hp comes from the part's `hpShare` (say what that gives in
  darts of each type in HANDOFF); the two-parry break stays as well.
  The `shieldBoss` (the orange weak point on it) the same.
- Tests: an F01 with its club shot off has full health; a club taking
  more damage than the F01's health does not kill it; a sword the same
  for the F07; enough darts into the F11's shield from the front break
  it off and the next front dart hurts the Seawall; fewer do not; the
  parry break still works.
