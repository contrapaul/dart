## What to build, in stages (commit each)

1. **The document and undo.** `src/editor/doc.ts`, pure (no DOM, no
   Three.js): holds a `Level`, and every change is a command (add, remove,
   change fields of one or more prims) on one undo list with undo and redo,
   plus a change listener. A new level is the smallest valid one: a 32 by
   32 m floor prim, the player start, an exit, the default sky from
   `data/editor.json`. Ids are unique (`box-1`, `ramp-2`, ...). Tested.
2. **The page.** `editor.html` and `src/editor/main.ts`: a full window
   three.js view with a floor grid (`data/editor.json` `grid`), the scene's
   lights, the level's shell boxes and pieces drawn read only, every prim
   drawn from `primGeometry` and `primMatrix`. Fly camera: hold right mouse
   to look, WASD to move, Q/E down/up, Shift faster; F frames the
   selection. Layout of design/EDITOR.md section 4: top bar, left outliner,
   right inspector, bottom palette. Plain DOM and CSS, no UI library.
3. **Place and edit.** The palette has the five shapes. Pick one, click in
   the view, it lands on the surface under the cursor (raycast), snapped to
   the grid. Click selects, Shift+click adds. W, E, R switch three.js
   `TransformControls` (import from `three/examples/jsm/controls/`) to
   move, rotate, scale, snapped to `grid` and `rotateStep`, G toggles snap.
   A scale changes `size`, never a mesh scale. Ctrl+D duplicates, Delete
   deletes, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y (and Ctrl+Shift+Z) undo and redo. The
   inspector edits id, position, size, rotation, colour (a colour input
   plus the `palette` swatches and the last six used), behaviour, steps for
   stairs, hazard fields (look from `data/hazards.json`, dps, slow) and
   hit points for breakable, and hidden. A hazard prim draws see-through in
   its look's colour. The outliner lists prims by behaviour with an eye
   (hide while editing, not saved) and click to select. Keys are ignored
   while typing in an input.
4. **Save, open, export, import, play.** Every change autosaves (debounced
   half a second) with `putLevel`; the top bar shows Saved. New, Open (the
   `listLevels` list, newest first, with delete), Export (downloads
   `<name>.dart.json`), Import (a file input, the file is checked with
   `validateLevel` and refused with its errors). Open also lists the
   built in levels (`data/levels/*.json`) to open as a copy. Play opens
   `arena.html?level=local:<key>` in the same tab; the browser's back
   button returns. The sky inspector (when nothing is selected): gradient
   colours, or an uploaded image scaled down to at most
   `skyImageWidth` by `skyImageHeight` on a canvas and stored as a JPEG
   data: URL in `level.sky`. The view shows the sky.
5. Show counts against `data/editor.json` `limits` in the top bar, and refuse
   to add a prim past `limits.prims`.
